# 100 Rainy Day Activities for Kids and Families — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

The groups matter because kids' energy changes through the day: start active, go creative in the middle, and end quiet. A rainy day with one anchor activity per part of the day — morning, afternoon, evening — feels full without feeling scheduled. Keep the list where you can reach it on a grey morning.
