## Keyword research briefs

1. Act as an SEO keyword researcher. For the topic [topic], list 15 primary and long-tail keywords with estimated search intent (informational, commercial, or transactional) and a difficulty score from 1 to 10. Format as a table with columns: Keyword, Intent, Difficulty, and Suggested use.
2. Given the seed keyword [seed keyword], generate 10 semantic keyword clusters, each with 5 related terms, and label each cluster with a proposed content angle and target search intent.
3. You are an SEO strategist. Compare search intent for the keyword [keyword] across the top 5 ranking pages and tell me whether the intent is informational, commercial, or transactional, with evidence for your classification.
4. Build a keyword map for a website about [niche]: group these 30 keywords [paste list] into pillar topics and supporting subtopics, and note which keyword should be the primary target for each group.
5. Act as a search analyst. For the keyword [keyword], list 8 People Also Ask style questions a real searcher would have before, during, and after their main search, ordered by stage of the buyer journey.
6. Analyze the keyword gap for [topic]: suggest 12 long-tail keywords with low competition that a new site with low domain authority could realistically rank for in under 6 months, and explain in one line why each is winnable.
7. Create a seasonal keyword brief for [topic]: identify 8 queries that spike seasonally, the months they peak, and what content format (guide, checklist, comparison) matches each query's intent.
8. You are an SEO copywriter. Turn this broad topic [topic] into 6 specific content briefs, each with one primary keyword, 3 secondary keywords, search intent, and a working title under 60 characters.
9. For the transactional keyword [keyword], list the 5 trust signals (reviews, comparisons, pricing, guarantees, case studies) searchers expect on the ranking pages, and draft a brief section outline that covers all of them.
10. Act as an SEO auditor. Given my target keyword [keyword] and my draft title [title], rate title-keyword alignment from 1 to 10 and rewrite the title 3 ways to improve exact-match clarity without sounding robotic.

## Outline briefs
