## Weekly home cleaning schedule template

### Monday — Kitchen reset
- Wipe counters and stovetop
- Clean the sink and faucet
- Take out trash and recycling
- Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
- Mop the kitchen floor

### Tuesday — Bathrooms
- Clean toilets, sinks, and showers or tubs
- Wipe mirrors and faucets
- Replace towels and hand towels
- Mop bathroom floors
- Restock soap, toilet paper, and supplies

### Wednesday — Dusting and surfaces
- Dust shelves, tables, and electronics
- Wipe light switches and door handles
- Clean windowsills and mirrors
- Tidy one drawer or shelf
- Fluff and straighten cushions

### Thursday — Floors
- Vacuum all carpets and rugs
- Sweep and mop hard floors
- Shake out doormats
- Spot-clean stains you notice
- Empty the vacuum or dustbin

### Friday — Bedrooms and laundry
- Change bed sheets and pillowcases
- Put away clean laundry
- Start one load of towels or bedding
- Tidy nightstands and desks
- Vacuum bedroom floors

### Saturday — Living areas and extras
- Tidy the living room and entryway
- Clean one appliance in depth (fridge, oven, or microwave)
- Water plants and wipe plant pots
- Take out all trash and recycling
- Do a 15-minute whole-house pickup

### Sunday — Rest and prep
- Light tidy only: put things back where they belong
- Prep cleaning supplies for the week
- Plan next week's deep-clean task
- Rest: the schedule works because Sunday is light

## Monthly deep-clean tasks (one per week, rotating)

Week 1: Clean the refrigerator inside and out
Week 2: Wash windows inside
Week 3: Clean oven and kitchen exhaust filters
Week 4: Vacuum under furniture and wash curtains

## Daily 10-minute habits

- Make beds every morning
- Wash dishes or load the dishwasher after meals
- Wipe the bathroom sink after the morning routine
- Put shoes, bags, and keys in their place on arrival
- One quick evening pickup before bed

## Rules that keep the schedule working

1. Small daily habits prevent weekend cleaning marathons.
2. If a day is missed, skip it and continue — do not try to catch up everything at once.
3. Split tasks among household members; one person should not carry the whole schedule.
4. Keep supplies in one caddy so cleaning starts in seconds, not after a search.
5. Review the schedule monthly and adjust tasks to your home's real needs.
