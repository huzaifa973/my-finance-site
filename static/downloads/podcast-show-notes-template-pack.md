# Podcast Show Notes Template Pack — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

Four complete show notes templates. Copy a template into your CMS and fill in the bracketed fields for each episode.

## Template 1 — Standard episode show notes

```
STANDARD EPISODE SHOW NOTES

Episode [NUMBER]: [EPISODE TITLE]

[2-3 sentence summary: what the episode covers and the key
outcome for the listener. Include your main keyword naturally.]

IN THIS EPISODE:
- [Key point 1]
- [Key point 2]
- [Key point 3]

TIMESTAMPS:
[00:00] - [Intro / cold open topic]
[04:32] - [First main segment]
[15:10] - [Second main segment]
[28:45] - [Third main segment / Q&A]
[39:20] - [Closing takeaways]

KEY TAKEAWAYS:
1. [Takeaway 1]
2. [Takeaway 2]
3. [Takeaway 3]

LINKS & RESOURCES MENTIONED:
- [Resource name](URL)
- [Resource name](URL)
- [Tool/book/article name](URL)

CALL TO ACTION:
[Subscribe/follow prompt] — [Leave a review prompt] —
[Join the newsletter/community prompt]

NEXT EPISODE: [Teaser for next episode]
```

## Template 2 — Guest interview show notes

```
GUEST INTERVIEW SHOW NOTES

Episode [NUMBER]: [EPISODE TITLE] with [GUEST NAME]

[2-3 sentence summary: who the guest is, their credibility,
and the core insight of the conversation.]

ABOUT [GUEST NAME]:
[3-4 sentence bio: who they are, what they're known for,
notable credentials or achievements.]

WHERE TO FIND [GUEST NAME]:
- Website: [URL]
- [Twitter/LinkedIn/Instagram]: [URL]
- [Book/product/newsletter]: [URL]

CONVERSATION HIGHLIGHTS:
[00:00] - [Intro and guest background]
[06:15] - [First big topic]
[18:40] - [Second big topic]
[32:05] - [Rapid-fire / personal questions]
[41:30] - [Guest's final advice]

QUOTABLE MOMENTS:
"[Pull quote 1 — the line guests share on social media.]"
— [GUEST NAME]
"[Pull quote 2]"
— [GUEST NAME]

KEY TAKEAWAYS:
1. [Takeaway 1]
2. [Takeaway 2]
3. [Takeaway 3]

LINKS & RESOURCES:
- [Everything mentioned, with links]

FOLLOW THE SHOW: [Subscribe links for Apple/Spotify/YouTube]
```
