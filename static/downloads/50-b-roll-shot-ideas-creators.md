# 50 B-Roll Shot Ideas for Creators

Keep this list on your phone during shoots. Shoot each idea for 10+ seconds with
2 seconds of stillness at the start and end (editors need handles).

## Hands and process shots (12)

1. Overhead shot of hands typing on a keyboard, shallow depth of field
2. Close-up of hands assembling, building, or fixing something
3. Slow-motion pour: coffee, water, or paint hitting a surface
4. Hands flipping through a notebook or shuffling papers
5. Close-up of a pen writing the video's key phrase on paper
6. Hands plugging in cables or pressing buttons on a device
7. Cooking process: chopping, stirring, seasoning in close-up
8. Hands packing a bag, box, or suitcase
9. Finger scrolling through a phone screen (screen visible)
10. Hands gesturing naturally during an explanation
11. Opening a package or unboxing a product
12. Hands adjusting camera, tripod, or lighting equipment

## Environment and lifestyle shots (10)

13. Wide shot of your workspace with warm window light, nobody in frame
14. Walking shot from behind, stabilized, through a doorway
15. Street scene: people walking, traffic, city lights at dusk
16. Cozy interior: couch, lamp, bookshelf in soft light
17. Nature establishing shot: trees, sky, water, mountains
18. Coffee shop or coworking space ambiance
19. Night city: neon signs, light trails, reflections
20. Your desk from the side, monitor glowing, room dark
21. Window with rain drops or sunlight streaming through
22. Aerial-style high angle of a room or street (phone held high or drone)

## Detail and texture close-ups (10)

23. Macro shot of texture: wood grain, fabric, keyboard keys, leaves
24. Rack focus from a blurred foreground object to the subject
25. Steam rising from a mug in backlit morning light
26. Water droplets on glass or a leaf
27. Close-up of eyes, then rack focus to what they're looking at
28. Slow-motion detail: dust in light, hair moving, fabric falling
29. Product detail: logo, stitching, screen, buttons in macro
30. Clock hands ticking or a watch face in close-up
31. Pages of a book turning in close-up
32. Fire, candles, or fairy lights bokeh in the background

## Movement and transition shots (10)

33. Whip pan transition: fast pan that blurs into the next scene
34. Walk-by transition: walk past the camera to wipe the frame
35. Door opening toward camera, revealing the next scene
36. Object wipe: pass a book or hand close to the lens
37. Spin transition: rotate the camera 360 degrees
38. Match cut setup: same framing, different location or time
39. Zoom punch-in on the subject's face for emphasis
40. Drone-style rise: start low, lift smoothly upward
41. Passing through a crowd or trees (foreground wipes)
42. Light flick: cover the lens, uncover on the beat

## Evergreen fillers (8)

43. Screen recording of the exact clicks you describe in narration
44. Time-lapse of clouds, traffic, or a workspace over an hour
45. Calendar pages or a planner being filled in
46. Phone notifications popping up on a desk
47. Shoes walking on different surfaces (pavement, grass, wood)
48. Sunset or sunrise time-lapse through a window
49. Bookshelf pan: slow horizontal move across book spines
50. Hands holding the product up to the camera, rotating slowly

## Quick shooting tips

- Shoot 10+ seconds per clip — you can trim, but you can't stretch.
- Get a wide AND a close-up of the same subject; pairs cut together perfectly.
- Lock exposure before rolling to avoid flicker in indoor light.
- Name clips descriptively when importing: `broll-hands-typing-overhead.mp4`.
