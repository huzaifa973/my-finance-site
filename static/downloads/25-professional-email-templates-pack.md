# 25 Professional Email Templates Pack

Replace every [bracket] with your specifics before sending. Keep emails under
125 words whenever possible: context, ask, next step.

## Follow-ups and nudges (5)

### 1. Post-meeting follow-up
Subject: Great meeting you — [topic] next steps
Hi [Name],
Great speaking today about [topic]. As promised, [attached/linked item].
Next step on my side: [action] by [date]. Could you [their action] by [date]?
Thanks,
[Your name]

### 2. Gentle deadline nudge
Subject: Quick nudge: [deliverable] due [date]
Hi [Name],
Just a friendly reminder that [deliverable] is due [date].
Let me know if anything is blocking you — happy to help.
Best,
[Your name]

### 3. Re-engaging a cold contact
Subject: Catching up — [shared context]
Hi [Name],
It's been a while since [last contact context]. I saw [recent news about them] — congratulations!
I'd love to catch up. Are you free for a quick call [timeframe]?
Best,
[Your name]

### 4. Following up on a proposal
Subject: [Proposal name] — any questions?
Hi [Name],
Wanted to check in on the [proposal name] I sent on [date].
Happy to walk through any part of it or adjust the [scope/pricing/timeline].
Would [day] work for a 15-minute call?
Thanks,
[Your name]

### 5. Closing the loop
Subject: Closing the loop on [topic]
Hi [Name],
I haven't heard back on [topic], so I'll assume the timing isn't right and close this out on my end.
If things change, just reply — happy to pick it back up.
Best,
[Your name]

## Introductions and networking (4)

### 6. Requesting an introduction
Subject: Quick intro request
Hi [Name],
I'd love an intro to [person] at [company] about [specific reason].
Happy to draft a forwardable note to make it easy. No worries at all if the timing isn't right.
Thanks so much,
[Your name]

### 7. Cold outreach that gets replies
Subject: [Specific compliment or observation]
Hi [Name],
[One genuine, specific sentence about their work.]
I [do X] and thought [specific reason it connects]. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat [timeframe]?
Either way, keep up the great work on [project].
Best,
[Your name]

### 8. Thanking someone for an intro
Subject: Thank you for the intro!
Hi [Name],
Thank you for connecting me with [person] — we [spoke/met] and [outcome].
I really appreciate you vouching for me.
[Your name]

### 9. Following up after networking
Subject: Great meeting you at [event]
Hi [Name],
Really enjoyed our chat about [topic] at [event].
As mentioned, [follow-through item]. Let's keep in touch — [suggest next step].
Best,
[Your name]

## Requests and asks (5)

### 10. Asking for a favor
Subject: Quick favor?
Hi [Name],
I hope you're well. I'm [context in one line] and could use your help with [specific ask].
It would take about [time estimate]. Would you be open to it?
Completely understand if you're swamped — no pressure.
Thanks,
[Your name]

### 11. Requesting information
Subject: Question about [topic]
Hi [Name],
I'm working on [project] and you came to mind as the expert on [topic].
Could you point me to [specific information]? Even a link would be a huge help.
Thanks in advance,
[Your name]

### 12. Asking for a deadline extension
Subject: Request: extend [deliverable] deadline to [new date]
Hi [Name],
I'm writing about [deliverable], currently due [date]. Due to [brief honest reason], I'd like to request an extension to [new date].
[What you've completed so far, to show progress.]
I understand if this isn't possible — please let me know.
Thanks,
[Your name]

### 13. Asking for a referral or testimonial
Subject: Would you write a short testimonial?
Hi [Name],
Working with you on [project] was a highlight. Would you be open to writing 2–3 sentences about the experience? I'd feature it [where].
To make it easy, here are prompts: [prompt 1], [prompt 2].
Thank you,
[Your name]

### 14. Requesting feedback
Subject: Your feedback on [item]?
Hi [Name],
I've [created/drafted] [item] and would value your honest feedback — especially on [specific aspect].
No rush: [date] would be plenty. Here's the link: [link].
Thanks,
[Your name]

## Apologies and fixes (4)

### 15. Owning a mistake
Subject: My mistake on [issue] — here's the fix
Hi [Name],
I want to own this: [what went wrong, specifically].
Here's what I'm doing to fix it: [action]. It won't happen again because [change].
I'm sorry for [impact on them].
[Your name]

### 16. Apologizing for a missed deadline
Subject: Sorry — [deliverable] delayed to [new date]
Hi [Name],
I'm sorry: [deliverable] won't be ready by [original date] because [brief reason].
New committed date: [date]. [What you're doing to prevent repeats.]
I understand this impacts [their plan] and I apologize.
[Your name]

### 17. Correcting a wrong send
Subject: Please ignore my last email — corrected version
Hi [Name],
Please disregard my previous email about [topic] — it contained [error].
The correct information is: [correction].
Sorry for the confusion,
[Your name]

### 18. Apologizing for a delayed reply
Subject: Re: [original subject] — sorry for the delay
Hi [Name],
Apologies for the slow reply on [topic]. [One-line reason, no excuses.]
To answer your question: [answer]. [Next step if any.]
Thanks for your patience,
[Your name]

## Scheduling and logistics (4)

### 19. Requesting a meeting
Subject: 20 minutes to discuss [topic]?
Hi [Name],
I'd like 20 minutes to discuss [topic] — specifically [agenda in one line].
I'm free [option 1] or [option 2]. Do either work, or suggest a time?
Thanks,
[Your name]

### 20. Rescheduling
Subject: Need to move our [date/time] meeting
Hi [Name],
I need to reschedule our [date/time] meeting about [topic] — [brief reason].
Would [option 1] or [option 2] work instead? Sorry for the shuffle.
Best,
[Your name]

### 21. Confirming a meeting
Subject: Confirmed: [topic] on [date/time]
Hi [Name],
Confirming our meeting on [date] at [time] ([timezone]) about [topic].
Agenda: [2–3 bullet points]. [Meeting link / location.]
See you then,
[Your name]

### 22. Sending an agenda
Subject: Agenda for [meeting] on [date]
Hi [Name],
Ahead of our [date] meeting, here's the agenda:
1. [Item] ([owner])
2. [Item] ([owner])
Please add anything I've missed.
Thanks,
[Your name]

## Closings and courtesy (3)

### 23. Thank-you note
Subject: Thank you for [specific thing]
Hi [Name],
Thank you for [specific thing]. It [specific impact it had].
I really appreciate [them/their time/their help].
Best,
[Your name]

### 24. Congratulations
Subject: Congratulations on [achievement]!
Hi [Name],
I just heard about [achievement] — congratulations! [One genuine sentence about why it's impressive.]
Wishing you continued success,
[Your name]

### 25. Out-of-office reply
Subject: Out of office until [date]
Thanks for your email. I'm out of the office until [date] with limited access to email.
For urgent matters, contact [name] at [email/phone].
I'll respond to everything else when I return.
Best,
[Your name]
