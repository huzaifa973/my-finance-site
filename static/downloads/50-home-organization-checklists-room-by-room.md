50 Home Organization Checklists, Room by Room — free resource from DollarWise

1. Entryway: clear the console table and keep only daily essentials on it
2. Entryway: set up a tray or bowl for keys, wallet, and sunglasses
3. Entryway: add hooks for coats and bags, one per household member
4. Entryway: place a shoe rack or basket by the door
5. Entryway: sort the coat closet and donate what nobody wears
6. Kitchen: clear counters to only daily-use appliances
7. Kitchen: declutter the junk drawer into small labeled compartments
8. Kitchen: sort the pantry by category and toss expired food
9. Kitchen: label pantry containers and put everyday items at eye level
10. Kitchen: organize pots, pans, and lids so every lid has a match
11. Kitchen: sort the fridge, toss expired items, and group by type
12. Kitchen: set up a cleaning-supply zone under the sink
13. Kitchen: organize the spice drawer or rack alphabetically
14. Kitchen: create a lunch-packing station with containers and bags
15. Living room: remove everything that does not belong in the room
16. Living room: organize remote controls into one basket or tray
17. Living room: sort books and media, donating what you will not re-read
18. Living room: set up a charging station for devices
19. Living room: add baskets for blankets and kids' toys
20. Living room: clear surfaces to one or two decorative items each
21. Bedroom: clear the nightstand to lamp, book, and water only
22. Bedroom: sort the dresser drawers by category, one drawer at a time
23. Bedroom: organize the closet by type, then by color
24. Bedroom: store out-of-season clothes in labeled bins
25. Bedroom: set up a laundry hamper system for lights and darks
26. Bedroom: clear under the bed to storage bins only
27. Bathroom: toss expired medicines, makeup, and toiletries
28. Bathroom: group daily products in one caddy or tray
29. Bathroom: organize the medicine cabinet by person and purpose
30. Bathroom: add hooks or a rack for towels, one per person
31. Bathroom: set up a cleaning kit kept in the bathroom itself
32. Kids' room: sort toys into keep, donate, and broken piles
33. Kids' room: label toy bins with pictures for younger kids
34. Kids' room: set up a homework station with supplies in reach
35. Kids' room: rotate toys — store half and swap monthly
36. Kids' room: create a display shelf for art and rotate it
37. Home office: clear the desk to computer, notebook, and one pen cup
38. Home office: sort papers into action, file, and shred piles
39. Home office: set up a filing system for bills, tax, and records
40. Home office: organize cables with clips and labels
41. Home office: back up digital files and clear the desktop
42. Laundry room: label detergent, stain remover, and supplies
43. Laundry room: set up a lost-sock basket and a mending pile
44. Laundry room: add a drying rack and a folding surface
45. Garage: sort tools onto a pegboard or labeled wall storage
46. Garage: clear the floor — everything on shelves or hooks
47. Garage: create zones: tools, garden, sports, and seasonal
48. Garage: donate or dispose of broken items and duplicates
49. Whole house: set up a donation box and empty it monthly
50. Whole house: do a five-minute nightly reset in every room
