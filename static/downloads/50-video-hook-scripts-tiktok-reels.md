## How to use each style

- Curiosity hooks open a loop the viewer needs closed — always deliver the payoff you promise.
- Contrarian hooks earn attention fast but need a real argument behind them, not just shock.
- Tutorial hooks work best when the [short time] is specific: "in 47 seconds" beats "quickly".
- Storytelling hooks build trust — use them for personal brands and behind-the-scenes content.
- List hooks are the most skimmable — pair them with on-screen numbers for maximum retention.
