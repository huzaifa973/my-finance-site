# 50 Resume Bullet Point Formulas That Show Impact — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

Use each formula as a skeleton: keep the structure, replace the bracketed parts with your facts. Every formula follows: strong action verb + what you did + measurable result.

## Revenue and growth

1. Grew [metric] from [X] to [Y] in [timeframe] by [action]. Example: Grew monthly recurring revenue from $40K to $68K in 9 months by launching a tiered pricing model.
2. Generated $[amount] in [revenue type] through [strategy]. Example: Generated $1.2M in new pipeline through outbound campaigns targeting mid-market SaaS accounts.
3. Increased [metric] by [X]% year over year via [initiative]. Example: Increased email-driven sales by 34% year over year via segmented lifecycle campaigns.
4. Launched [product/feature] that acquired [X] customers in [timeframe]. Example: Launched a self-serve checkout that acquired 1,800 customers in its first quarter.
5. Closed [X] deals worth $[amount] with an average cycle of [timeframe]. Example: Closed 22 enterprise deals worth $3.4M with an average cycle of 45 days.
6. Expanded into [market/segment], adding [X] accounts and $[amount] ARR. Example: Expanded into the healthcare segment, adding 14 accounts and $520K ARR.
7. Lifted conversion from [X]% to [Y]% by [change]. Example: Lifted landing page conversion from 2.1% to 4.6% by rewriting the offer and adding social proof.
8. Grew [audience] from [X] to [Y] followers/subscribers in [timeframe]. Example: Grew the company newsletter from 8K to 41K subscribers in 12 months.
9. Negotiated [X] partnerships contributing $[amount] in annual revenue. Example: Negotiated 6 channel partnerships contributing $890K in annual revenue.
10. Ranked [content/campaign] to [position], driving [X] organic leads per month. Example: Ranked a buyer's guide to #2 on Google, driving 300 organic leads per month.

## Cost and efficiency

11. Cut [cost type] by [X]% ($[amount]) through [action]. Example: Cut cloud hosting costs by 38% ($96K/year) through rightsizing and reserved instances.
12. Reduced [process] time from [X] to [Y] by [improvement]. Example: Reduced invoice processing time from 6 days to 1 day by automating approvals.
13. Automated [task], saving [X] hours per [week/month]. Example: Automated weekly reporting, saving 12 hours per week across the team.
14. Consolidated [X] [tools/vendors] into [Y], saving $[amount] annually. Example: Consolidated 7 analytics tools into 2, saving $54K annually.
15. Decreased error rate from [X]% to [Y]% by [fix]. Example: Decreased order error rate from 4.2% to 0.8% by adding barcode verification.
16. Renegotiated [contract/vendor], lowering spend by [X]% with no service loss. Example: Renegotiated the logistics contract, lowering spend by 21% with no service loss.
17. Streamlined [workflow], eliminating [X] manual steps. Example: Streamlined onboarding, eliminating 11 manual steps and 3 handoff delays.
18. Reduced waste/scrap by [X]%, recovering $[amount] per [period]. Example: Reduced material waste by 17%, recovering $38K per quarter.
19. Shortened [cycle] from [X] to [Y] days, freeing [resource]. Example: Shortened the hiring cycle from 62 to 34 days, freeing managers for core work.
20. Delivered [project] [X]% under budget ($[amount] saved). Example: Delivered the office relocation 18% under budget ($72K saved).
