# 50 Podcast Interview Questions Pack — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

Use these as starting points, not a script. The best interviews come from listening to the answer and asking the follow-up nobody wrote down.

## Origin stories

1. What first pulled you into this field?
2. Was there a single moment when you knew this was your path?
3. What did your early days look like, before anyone knew your name?
4. Who believed in you before you had results to show?
5. What would you tell yourself on day one if you could?

## Craft and process

6. Walk us through a typical day when you are deep in a project.
7. What part of your process surprises people the most?
8. How do you know when something is finished — or finished enough?
9. What tool or habit could you never give up?
10. Where do your best ideas usually come from?

## Challenges and setbacks

11. What was the hardest period of your career, and what carried you through?
12. Tell us about a failure that later turned out to be useful.
13. What is a mistake you made that you now warn others about?
14. Have you ever wanted to quit? What stopped you?
15. What criticism changed your approach for the better?

## Decisions and turning points

16. What was the riskiest decision you have made, and how did it turn out?
17. How do you decide what to say yes to — and what to refuse?
18. Was there a fork in the road where you chose the harder path?
19. What opportunity did you turn down that you still think about?
20. How has your definition of success changed over time?

## People and influences

21. Who has shaped your thinking more than anyone else?
22. What is the best advice you ever received — and did you follow it?
23. Who would you love to learn from, given the chance?
24. What do you look for in the people you work with?
25. Is there a mentor whose voice still guides you today?

## Money and business

26. How do you think about pricing your work fairly?
27. What was your first paid project, and what did it teach you?
28. How do you handle slow months or dry spells?
29. What financial lesson do you wish you had learned sooner?
30. When did you first feel financially secure in this work?

## Audience and community

31. How do you stay connected to the people who follow your work?
32. What does your audience misunderstand most about what you do?
33. How do you handle negative feedback in public?
34. What has your community taught you that no course could?
35. How do you grow without losing what made people care?

## Future and trends

36. Where do you think this industry is headed in the next five years?
37. What trend excites you, and what trend worries you?
38. What skill will matter most for newcomers in the next decade?
39. If you started today, what would you do differently?
40. What are you working on next that you are excited about?

## Personal life

41. What do you do to switch off completely?
42. How do you protect time for family and friends in a busy career?
43. What book, film, or song changed how you see the world?
44. What is a small daily habit that makes a big difference for you?
45. What are you most proud of outside of work?

## Quick-fire round

46. One word to describe your work?
47. Early bird or night owl?
48. What is the last thing you Googled?
49. Coffee or tea — and how do you take it?
50. What is one thing on your bucket list?
