# 50 Podcast Episode Title Templates — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

The templates are grouped by episode type because a title that works for an interview (“How [guest] built [thing]”) fails for a debate (“Should we [controversial thing]?”). Keep titles under sixty characters where possible — long titles truncate in most players — and put the most interesting word first, not last.

INTERVIEWS (1-10)
How [guest] built [thing] from nothing
Inside [place]: a conversation with [guest]
What [guest] knows about [topic] that you do not
[Guest] on [topic], failure, and starting over
The [guest] story: from [start] to [success]
Ask [guest] anything: [topic] edition
[Guest] explains [complex topic] simply
A day in the life of [guest]
How [guest] survived [hard event]
[Guest]'s [number] rules for [goal]
STORIES (11-20)
The story of [event] nobody told you
What really happened at [place]
The [adjective] story of [person]
How [thing] almost never happened
The night everything changed for [person]
Lost and found: the [object] story
The mystery of [place or event]
A story about [topic] you will not forget
When [event] went wrong — and what followed
The untold history of [thing]
HOW-TOS (21-30)
How to [skill] in [timeframe]
A beginner's guide to [topic]
The [number]-step system for [goal]
How to start [thing] with no experience
Master [skill]: the complete walkthrough
How I learned [skill] in [timeframe]
The easy way to [goal] (finally)
How to fix [common problem] for good
Your first [thing]: everything you need
How to [skill] without [common obstacle]
DEBATES AND OPINIONS (31-40)
Should we [controversial idea]? A debate
Is [trend] actually worth it?
My honest take on [topic]
Unpopular opinion: [opinion]
The case for [idea] — and against it
Hot take: [provocative statement]
We disagree about [topic] — hear us out
Is [thing] dead? A frank discussion
The [topic] debate, settled (mostly)
Why everyone is wrong about [topic]
SOLO AND LIST EPISODES (41-50)
The [number] mistakes every [audience] makes
What I learned from [experience]
[Number] lessons from [time period]
My [number] favourite [things] right now
Answering your questions about [topic]
What I would do differently about [thing]
The [number] tools I use every day
A letter to my younger self about [topic]
This week in [field]: what matters
Quick answers: your [topic] questions
