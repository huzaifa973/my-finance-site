# Sleep Schedule Template Pack for Shift Workers

Shift work breaks sleep in predictable ways — and a schedule built for a 9-to-5 life makes it worse. This pack gives ten ready-made sleep templates, one per shift pattern: nights, early mornings, swing shifts, rotating rosters, and 12-hour days and nights. Each template sets a sleep window, a wind-down start time, and the light rules that actually shift your body clock. It is written for nurses, factory workers, drivers, security staff, and anyone whose roster ignores the sun.

## How to use

1. Pick the items that fit your situation and ignore the rest — a template pack works best when you use ten items well, not all of them once.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder ([Business Name], [topic], [Name]) with your own details before publishing or sharing.
3. Save your customized version in one place so you can reuse it next time without starting over.
4. Review the result once in its real context — a listing, a page, or a reply — before moving on.
