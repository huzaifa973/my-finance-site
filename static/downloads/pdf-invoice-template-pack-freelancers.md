for [Amount], which was due on [Due Date].

If payment has already been sent, please disregard this message. If not,
you can pay here: [Payment link / bank details].

Per our agreement, a [1.5]% monthly late fee applies to overdue balances.
Please let me know if there's anything you need from my side to
process this.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
