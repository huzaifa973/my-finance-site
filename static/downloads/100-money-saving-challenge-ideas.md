# 100 Money-Saving Challenge Ideas — Checklist
Free from DollarWise — check off each challenge as you complete it.

## No-Spend Challenges (1-20)

- [ ] No-spend weekend: spend nothing for 48 hours except pre-planned essentials, and move what you would have spent into savings
- [ ] No-spend workweek: pack every meal and skip all shops Monday to Friday, logging each avoided purchase
- [ ] No-spend month: one full calendar month of essentials-only spending, with a written list of allowed exceptions
- [ ] No-spend category month: pick your worst category (eating out, clothes, gadgets) and spend zero there for 30 days
- [ ] 24-hour rule challenge: wait a full day before any non-essential purchase for two weeks straight
- [ ] Cash-only week: withdraw a fixed amount and spend nothing digitally — when the cash is gone, spending stops
- [ ] No delivery week: zero food delivery or takeout for seven days, cook everything at home
- [ ] No new clothes month: wear, repair, or swap what you own for 30 days, no new purchases
- [ ] Free-weekend challenge: plan two full days of fun using only free activities
- [ ] Pantry-only week: eat only what is already in your kitchen for seven days
- [ ] No-coffee-shop month: make every coffee at home for 30 days and bank the savings
- [ ] Screen-free spending freeze: no online shopping for two weeks, delete the apps temporarily
- [ ] Gift-spending pause: for one month, give experiences or handmade gifts only, zero store-bought
- [ ] No-impulse week: every unplanned purchase goes on a list instead, review the list after seven days
- [ ] One-in-one-out month: for every item you buy, one must leave the house, donated or sold
- [ ] Low-spend February: a light version of no-spend month with a small daily allowance
- [ ] No-spend date nights: four creative dates in a month with zero spending, picnics and stargazing count
- [ ] Zero-waste grocery week: buy only what you will definitely eat, and waste nothing
- [ ] No-taxi week: walk, cycle, or take public transport everywhere for seven days
- [ ] Social-spending pause: two weeks of free hangouts only, be honest with friends about it

## 52-Week and Round-Up Challenges (21-40)

- [ ] 52-week challenge: save an amount matching the week number each week — 1 in week one, 52 in week fifty-two
- [ ] Reverse 52-week challenge: start big and save 52 in week one, counting down so the final weeks feel easy
- [ ] Round-up challenge: round every purchase up to the next whole unit and transfer the difference to savings at week end
- [ ] 52-week mini: save half the standard amounts for a gentler year-long habit
- [ ] Weekly bump challenge: start at 5 per week and add 5 each week for 12 weeks
- [ ] Coin jar challenge: save every coin you receive for three months, count and deposit at the end
- [ ] Found-money challenge: save every unexpected windfall — refunds, rebates, birthday cash — for six months
- [ ] Pay-yourself raise: every time you get a raise or bonus, save the entire increase for one year
- [ ] Daily dollar challenge: put aside 1 every day for 100 days, then 2 a day for the next 100
- [ ] Weather challenge: save an amount matching the day's high temperature each week for a month
- [ ] Bill-round challenge: round every bill payment up to the next 10 and save the difference
- [ ] Spare-change app challenge: use a round-up feature for 90 days without touching the balance
- [ ] Envelope-a-day: number 100 envelopes 1 to 100, draw one daily and stash that amount
- [ ] 12-week sprint: save a fixed ambitious amount every week for exactly 12 weeks
- [ ] Birthday countdown: save weekly for the 12 weeks before a big expense like holidays
- [ ] Penny-a-day double: save 1 cent on day one and double it daily for 30 days, if your budget allows
- [ ] No-spend-day counter: mark each no-spend day on a calendar and save 5 per marked day monthly
- [ ] Percentage bump: raise your automatic savings rate by 1 percent each month for six months
- [ ] 26-week payday challenge: save an amount matching the pay period number every payday for half a year
- [ ] Quarter challenge: save every 25-cent coin you touch for 90 days

## Food and Grocery Challenges (41-60)

- [ ] Pantry challenge week: eat only what is already in your pantry and freezer for seven days, buying only fresh milk or bread if needed
- [ ] Meal-prep Sunday: cook all weekday lunches at once and compare the cost to one week of bought lunches
- [ ] Grocery budget cap: set a strict weekly grocery limit 20 percent below your average and stick to it
- [ ] Store-brand swap month: buy the store brand of everything for 30 days and save the price gap
- [ ] Meatless week: cook vegetarian for seven days and bank what you normally spend on meat
- [ ] One-pot week: cook only one-pot meals for seven days to cut ingredients and energy use
- [ ] Leftovers-only lunches: eat leftovers for lunch every workday for two weeks
- [ ] Grocery list lockdown: shop strictly from a list for a month, zero unlisted items
- [ ] Farmers-market challenge: buy produce at a local market for a month and compare quality per cost
- [ ] Bulk-buy basics: buy rice, beans, and oats in bulk once and track how many meals they cover
- [ ] Freezer inventory week: cook only from the freezer until it is empty, then restock smartly
- [ ] Snack-spending freeze: no packaged snacks for two weeks, make your own instead
- [ ] Dining-out budget: allow exactly two restaurant meals in a month, planned in advance
- [ ] Coffee math month: track every coffee purchase for 30 days, then brew at home for the next 30
- [ ] Batch-cooking Sunday: prepare and freeze ten portions of one staple meal
- [ ] Price-book challenge: record prices of 20 staples across two stores and shop the cheaper one
- [ ] Ugly-produce week: buy discounted imperfect produce and use it all creatively
- [ ] Spice-rack cooking: cook for a week using only spices and staples you already own
- [ ] Breakfast-at-home month: eat breakfast at home every day for 30 days, no exceptions
- [ ] Grow-one-herb challenge: grow basil or mint on a windowsill and stop buying it for three months

## Energy and Utility Challenges (61-80)

- [ ] Thermostat challenge: lower heating by 2 degrees or raise cooling by 2 degrees for a month and bank the bill difference
- [ ] Vampire-power hunt: unplug every standby device for a week and measure the drop on your next bill
- [ ] Shorter-shower week: cut every shower to five minutes for seven days
- [ ] Laundry line-dry month: air-dry clothes instead of using the dryer for 30 days
- [ ] Lights-out evenings: use only one room of lighting after dark for two weeks
- [ ] Cold-wash month: wash all laundry in cold water for 30 days
- [ ] Full-load rule: run the dishwasher and washer only when completely full for a month
- [ ] Water-audit week: fix one drip or leak and shorten tap-running habits for seven days
- [ ] Peak-hour shift: move big appliance use to off-peak hours for one billing cycle
- [ ] LED swap sprint: replace every remaining old bulb with LED in one weekend
- [ ] Curtain climate control: use curtains to block heat or keep warmth for a month, track comfort
- [ ] Phone-charge audit: charge devices only to 80 percent and unplug chargers for two weeks
- [ ] One-fridge challenge: consolidate to a single fridge for a month if you run two
- [ ] Bill-compare hour: spend one hour comparing your utility plan to competitors and switch if cheaper
- [ ] Standby-strip week: plug entertainment gear into one power strip and switch it off nightly
- [ ] Oven-off week: cook without the oven for seven days, using stovetop, microwave, or no-cook meals
- [ ] Fan-first cooling: use fans before air conditioning for two weeks and note the bill change
- [ ] Insulation check: seal one drafty window or door with low-cost draft strips this month
- [ ] Meter-reading habit: read your meter weekly for a month to spot usage spikes early
- [ ] Gadget curfew: no device charging overnight for 30 days, charge during the day instead

## Subscription and Lifestyle Challenges (81-100)

- [ ] Subscription audit sprint: list every subscription, cancel one today, and redirect its cost to savings for three months
- [ ] Sell-one-thing week: list one unused item for sale each day for seven days and save every cent earned
- [ ] Declutter-for-cash month: sell or donate 30 items in 30 days, saving all proceeds
- [ ] Library-first month: borrow instead of buying books, movies, and magazines for 30 days
- [ ] DIY repair week: fix one broken item yourself using free online guides instead of replacing it
- [ ] Gift-budget envelope: set a fixed cash gift budget for the next occasion and do not exceed it
- [ ] Free-hobby month: explore one hobby that costs nothing — hiking, drawing, coding tutorials — for 30 days
- [ ] Carpool or transit week: share rides or use transit for every commute for seven days
- [ ] Insurance quote hour: get three competing quotes for one policy and switch if you save
- [ ] Bank-fee hunt: find every fee on your statements this month and eliminate or negotiate each one
- [ ] Phone-plan downgrade: switch to a cheaper plan matching your actual data use for three months
- [ ] Annual-billing switch: move one monthly subscription to annual billing and save the discount
- [ ] Free-trial purge: cancel every free trial before it converts this month
- [ ] Loyalty-program harvest: redeem all unused points and rewards this month instead of letting them expire
- [ ] Skill-swap week: trade a skill with a friend instead of paying for a service
- [ ] Mending month: repair clothes instead of replacing them for 30 days
- [ ] Entertainment budget night: one fixed cash amount per week for all fun spending, no cards
- [ ] Membership pause: freeze the gym or club membership for a month and work out free
- [ ] Price-negotiation day: call one provider — internet, phone, insurance — and ask for a better rate
- [ ] 30-day savings streak: save something, anything, every single day for 30 days and watch the habit form
