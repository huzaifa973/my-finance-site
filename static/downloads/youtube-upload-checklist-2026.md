# YouTube Upload Checklist 2026: 30 Steps Before You Hit Publish

You finished editing, the render is done, and the temptation is to hit publish and walk away. That is exactly how creators lose half a video's potential — a missing end screen, a thumbnail that never gets replaced, captions nobody adds, or a description box left empty. This 30-step YouTube upload checklist turns publishing into a repeatable 15-minute routine covering video quality, metadata that feeds search and suggested traffic, accessibility basics, and the first-24-hours promotion moves that actually move the needle.

## The 30 steps

1. Export at 1080p minimum (4K if you filmed in 4K) with a sensible bitrate — 8-12 Mbps for 1080p, 35-45 Mbps for 4K.
2. Watch the full video once at normal speed to catch glitches, typos, and audio dropouts.
3. Check audio levels: dialogue peaks around -12dB to -6dB, with no clipping at 0dB.
4. Add captions or subtitles (upload an SRT file) for accessibility and extra watch time.
5. Design a custom thumbnail: 1280x720, bold text of 4 words or fewer, high contrast, readable at small size.
6. Write the title: under 60 characters, main keyword near the front, curiosity plus clarity.
7. Write the description: hook in the first 150 characters, timestamps, links, and any disclosure.
8. Research 3-5 tags and choose your hashtags (only the first 3 show above the title).
9. Upload as Unlisted first so HD versions can finish processing before anyone watches.
10. Add the video to a relevant playlist so it earns suggested traffic from your own catalog.
11. Set the audience correctly: Made for Kids or not — this choice is legally binding.
12. Pick the right category and video language so YouTube routes it to the right viewers.
13. Add chapters via timestamps in the description (or the Chapters feature) for key moments.
14. Set an end screen pointing to your next-best video plus a subscribe button.
15. Add info cards at moments where viewers need context or a link.
16. Plan your pinned comment: a question or a timestamp to the best part of the video.
17. Choose visibility deliberately: Public on a schedule, or a Premiere for live chat hype.
18. Schedule the publish for when your audience is online (check Analytics, then Audience).
19. Verify the HD versions processed (1080p/4K badges in Studio) before announcing anywhere.
20. Reply to the first comments within the hour — early engagement compounds.
21. Share the video in ONE community where it genuinely fits (no spam, ever).
22. Feature the video on your channel page or in a series playlist if it belongs there.
23. Check real-time analytics at 24 hours: click-through rate and average view duration.
24. If CTR is under 4%, A/B test a new thumbnail and title with Test and Compare.
25. Link the video from your next relevant upload's description and end screen.
26. Translate the title and description if analytics show meaningful traffic from other languages.
27. File the video in your content tracker: link, date, topic, and performance notes.
28. Revisit the description every quarter and fix dead links or outdated offers.
29. Clip a 60-second highlight into a Short (or TikTok/Reel) that links back to the full video.
30. Write down ONE thing to improve next time — one lesson per video compounds fast.

## How to use this checklist

1. Finish steps 1-8 in your editor before opening the upload page — fixing audio or re-exporting after upload is painful.
2. Upload as Unlisted and work through steps 9-18 while the HD versions process in the background.
3. Schedule the publish for your peak audience hour (Analytics, then Audience), and run steps 19-22 in the first hour it is live.
4. At the 24-hour mark, check the real-time card for CTR and retention — steps 23-24 are the feedback loop that makes the checklist smarter with every upload.
5. Finish with steps 27-30 so each video feeds your archive, your Shorts pipeline, and your skills.

Run the same order every time and publishing stops feeling like a chore — it becomes the quiet system behind consistent growth. Grab the downloadable version below and tick items off as you go.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should I upload as Unlisted first?**

Yes. YouTube takes time to process the HD versions of your video, and publishing before the 1080p or 4K versions are ready means your first viewers — the ones the algorithm watches most closely — see a blurry upload. Upload as Unlisted, finish every setting, then go public once HD is done processing.

**What click-through rate should I aim for?**

For most channels, 4-10% is healthy. Below 4%, test a new thumbnail or title — YouTube's built-in Test and Compare tool makes this easy. Above 10%, you're doing great. Context matters more than the raw number: 6% across 100,000 impressions beats 15% across 2,000.

**How fast should I reply to comments?**

Within the first few hours if you can. Early commenters are your most engaged viewers, and replies spark the conversations that keep a video alive in the first 24 hours. A thoughtful pinned comment also gives new viewers something to respond to.
