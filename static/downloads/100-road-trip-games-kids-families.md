100 Road Trip Games for Kids and Families — free resource from DollarWise

1. License plate bingo — spot plates from as many states or regions as you can and mark them off a list
2. The alphabet game — find the letters A to Z in order on road signs, billboards, and plates
3. Twenty questions — one player thinks of a person, place, or thing and the rest ask yes-or-no questions
4. I spy — pick something visible from the car and give its color or first letter as a clue
5. The quiet game — whoever stays silent longest wins; giggles count as losing
6. Story chain — each person adds one sentence to a story, going around the car until it ends
7. Two truths and a lie — tell three facts about yourself and let everyone guess the lie
8. Name that tune — hum or sing the first few notes of a song and let others guess it
9. Animal alphabet — name an animal for each letter of the alphabet, going around the car
10. The rhyming game — pick a word and take turns naming words that rhyme with it
11. Would you rather — take turns offering two tricky choices and defending your pick
12. Counting cows — count cows on your side of the road; a cemetery on your side resets your count to zero
13. The movie game — name an actor, then the next player names a movie they were in, and so on
14. Song title game — sing a line from a song, and the next player must sing a song starting with its last word
15. Guess the song from the first line — read or sing only the opening line and let others guess
16. The category game — pick a category like animals or foods and name items alphabetically
17. Memory tray — study ten items on a tray for thirty seconds, then list as many as you remember
18. Fortunately, unfortunately — alternate sentences starting with fortunately and unfortunately to build a silly story
19. The humming game — hum a well-known tune while others guess the song
20. Word association — say a word, and the next player must instantly say the first word it brings to mind
21. The name game — name a famous person whose first name starts with the last letter of the previous name
22. Scavenger hunt — make a list of things to spot, like a red barn, a windmill, or a horse, and check them off
23. Punch buggy — call out and gently tap when you spot a classic compact car; no punching back
24. The telephone game — whisper a phrase around the car and compare the final version to the original
25. Odd one out — name three things and let others guess which one does not belong, and why
26. The picnic game — go around naming items to bring on a picnic, alphabetically, repeating all previous items
27. Guess the animal sound — imitate an animal and let others guess which one it is
28. The capital game — name a country and let the next player name its capital, then a new country
29. Road sign charades — act out a word or phrase silently while others guess
30. The color game — pick a color and take turns spotting things of that color before anyone else
31. Finish the lyric — sing a song up to a point, stop, and let someone else finish the line
32. The question game — hold a conversation where every sentence must be a question
33. Invent a country — design a fictional country together: name, flag, food, and one strange law
34. The grocery game — name a grocery item for each letter of the alphabet in order
35. Car karaoke — sing along loudly to a playlist, with bonus points for dramatic performance
36. The map game — pick a town on the map and invent a funny reason to visit it
37. Hot and cold — hide an imaginary object in the car and guide guesses with hotter and colder hints
38. The story title game — invent a book title and let the next player describe what the book is about
39. Count the colors — count how many cars of one color pass in five minutes, then switch colors
40. The superhero game — invent a superhero based on the next road sign you pass
41. Truth or dare, car edition — keep dares safe and seated, like singing a verse or doing an impression
42. The jobs game — guess what job a stranger in another car might have, based only on their car
43. Alphabet dinner — plan a meal where every dish starts with the next letter of the alphabet
44. The word ladder — change one letter of a word at a time to reach a target word
45. Guess the age — guess the age of buildings, trees, or bridges you pass and debate the clues
46. The band game — name a band, then the next band must start with the last letter of the previous one
47. Story dice — roll a die and add that many words to a shared story on your turn
48. The opposite game — answer every question with the opposite of what you mean
49. Memory lane — each person shares a favorite childhood road trip memory in turn
50. The spelling bee — take turns spelling tricky words, with harder words for older players
51. License plate phrases — make a funny phrase from the letters on a passing plate
52. The time game — guess how many minutes until the next town, and see who is closest
53. Animal charades — act out an animal silently while others guess, keeping seatbelts on
54. The food chain — name a food, and the next food must start with the last letter of the previous one
55. Guess the smell — describe a smell from childhood and let others guess what it was
56. The island game — name three things you would take to a desert island and explain why
57. Counting tunnels and bridges — keep score of tunnels, bridges, and rest stops along the route
58. The movie quote game — say a famous movie line and let others name the film
59. Invent a commercial — make up a silly advertisement for an everyday object in the car
60. The drawing game — describe a picture in words while another player draws it without looking
61. The accent game — read a road sign aloud in a funny accent and let others guess the accent
62. What am I — stick an imaginary label on your forehead and ask yes-or-no questions to guess it
63. The dream car game — describe your dream car in detail and let others add one ridiculous feature
64. Number plate math — add or multiply the numbers on a plate and race to the answer
65. The wish game — each person names one wish for the trip, and the group votes on the best one
66. Song roulette — shuffle a playlist and guess the artist before the first line is sung
67. The compliment game — go around giving each person a genuine compliment, one at a time
68. Guess the landmark — describe a famous landmark without naming it and let others guess
69. The backwards game — say a word and let the next player spell it backwards
70. The packing game — name one thing you packed and one thing you wish you had packed
71. The weather game — predict the weather at the next stop and see who is closest
72. Tell a tall tale — take turns exaggerating a true story until it becomes completely unbelievable
73. The color car game — guess the color of the next car to pass, with points for each correct guess
74. Name that smell — describe the smell of a place you have visited and let others guess the place
75. The alphabet animals — name an animal and a place it lives for each letter of the alphabet
76. The road trip playlist game — each person adds one song that fits the trip, explaining their choice
77. Guess the year — name an event or invention and let others guess the year it happened
78. The mime game — mime an everyday activity while others guess what it is
79. The counting game — count something together, like wind turbines, in rounds of ten
80. The future game — predict what the driver will see around the next bend
81. The animal alphabet race — race to name an animal for every letter before the next town
82. The sing-off — two teams sing alternate lines of the same song until one team forgets the words
83. The quiet alphabet — write the alphabet in the air with a finger while others guess the letter
84. The joke contest — take turns telling jokes, and the first person to laugh loses the round
85. The map dot game — close your eyes, point at the map, and invent a trip to that spot
86. The snack game — guess the flavor of a snack by taste alone, blindfolded at a rest stop
87. The mirror game — copy the facial expressions of the person next to you for one minute
88. The riddle race — take turns telling riddles, with a point for each one solved
89. The hat game — imagine what hat each person in another car might be wearing and why
90. The thank-you game — each person names one thing they are grateful for about the trip so far
91. The license plate story — invent a story about the driver of a car from its plate letters
92. The cloud game — look at clouds and describe the shapes you see, voting on the best one
93. The radio game — flip stations and invent a story linking the songs you hear
94. The rest stop race — at each stop, race to find three specific things, like a red door or a pine tree
95. The memory song — sing a song from memory, with a point lost for each forgotten word
96. The geography chain — name a place, and the next place must start with its last letter
97. The invention game — invent a gadget that would make road trips better and pitch it to the car
98. The echo game — repeat exactly what the previous player said, then add one new sentence
99. The favorite game — take turns naming favorites: food, movie, song, place, and why
100. The goodbye game — at the end of the trip, each person shares their favorite moment of the journey
