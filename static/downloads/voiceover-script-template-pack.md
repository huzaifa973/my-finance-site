## Voiceover script template pack

### How to use these templates

- [Direction] notes tell the voice actor how to read each line: pace, tone, and emphasis.
- [Pause] marks a short beat. [Beat] is shorter. Use them where a listener needs a moment.
- Replace ALL-CAPS placeholders with your own words, then read the script aloud twice before recording.

## Recording checklist

1. Warm up your voice: hum, stretch, and read a paragraph aloud.
2. Record in a quiet room with soft surfaces; closets full of clothes work well.
3. Keep the microphone 6 to 8 inches from your mouth, slightly off to one side.
4. Record each section twice and keep the better take.
5. Leave two seconds of silence at the start and end of every recording.
6. Listen back on headphones before you call it done.
