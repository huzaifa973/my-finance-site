2. Ride bikes along a riverside path and stop for ice cream.
3. Visit a farmers market and cook dinner from what you find.
4. Go birdwatching in a nearby park with a simple identification guide.
5. Have a sunrise walk and watch the town wake up.
6. Try fishing at a local lake, even if you catch nothing.
7. Explore a botanical garden and photograph your favorite plants.
8. Play a family game of soccer or frisbee in an open field.
9. Go on a nature scavenger hunt with a list made at home.
10. Kayak or paddle on calm water if rentals are available nearby.
11. Take a scenic drive with no destination and three snack stops.
12. Visit a pick-your-own farm and bake with the harvest.
13. Go stargazing away from city lights with a free sky app.
14. Try geocaching: a real-world treasure hunt using your phone.
15. Walk a historic neighborhood and read every plaque you pass.
16. Have a beach day with a sandcastle competition.
17. Go camping in the backyard with a tent, lanterns, and stories.
18. Visit a lake and skip stones while the sun sets.

20. Take a photography walk: each person shoots one theme.
