# 100 Out of Office Message Templates — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

The four tones exist because workplaces differ: a law firm wants formal, a startup wants friendly, a team that knows you well tolerates funny, and a busy inbox wants minimal. Each tone covers the same five situations — holiday, sick leave, conference, busy period, and limited access — so you always have a fitting option.

FORMAL — HOLIDAY
Thank you for your email. I am out of the office on annual leave until [date] and will respond on my return. For urgent matters, please contact [name] at [email].
Thank you for your message. I am currently on leave until [date] with limited access to email. Please direct urgent enquiries to [name] at [email].
I am out of the office until [date]. Your email will be attended to on my return. For immediate assistance, contact [name] at [email].
Thank you for contacting me. I am on annual leave until [date]. For time-sensitive matters, please reach [name] at [email].
I am currently away from the office until [date]. I will reply to your email upon my return. Urgent matters: [name], [email].
