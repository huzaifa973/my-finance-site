# 25 SEO Blog Post Outline Templates

Fill the [bracketed] slots with your keyword, topic, audience, and year. Each template
includes the heading skeleton, section prompts, and FAQ block.

## HOW-TO OUTLINES (7)

### Template 1 — Classic How-To
H1: How to [Task] ([Year] Guide for [Audience])
Intent note: the searcher wants a beginner-friendly walkthrough, not a product pitch
Intro: promise the outcome + who this is for + what they'll need
H2: What you need before you start
H2: How to [Task]: step-by-step
  H3: Step 1 — [First action]
  H3: Step 2 — [Second action]
  H3: Step 3 — [Final action]
H2: Common mistakes to avoid
H2: Frequently asked questions (4–6 questions)
Conclusion: recap the outcome + one next step

### Template 2 — Quick-Answer How-To
H1: How to [Task] in [Timeframe or Steps]
Intro: answer the question in the first 50 words (featured-snippet target)
H2: The short answer
H2: Detailed steps
  H3: Step 1 — [Action]
  H3: Step 2 — [Action]
H2: What to do if it doesn't work
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: one-line recap

### Template 3 — How-To With Tools
H1: How to [Task] With [Tool Name] ([Year])
Intro: why this tool + what the reader will achieve
H2: [Tool] pricing and plans (brief)
H2: Setting up [Tool] for [Task]
H2: [Task] walkthrough
  H3: [Stage 1]
  H3: [Stage 2]
H2: [Tool] tips most users miss
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: verdict on the tool + alternative if it's not a fit

### Template 4 — Troubleshooting How-To
H1: How to Fix [Problem] ([Number] Methods That Work)
Intro: acknowledge the frustration + promise tested fixes
H2: Why [Problem] happens
H2: Method 1 — [Quickest fix]
H2: Method 2 — [Deeper fix]
H2: Method 3 — [Last-resort fix]
H2: Preventing [Problem] in the future
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: which method to try first

### Template 5 — Beginner's Guide How-To
H1: [Topic] for Beginners: Everything You Need to Know
Intro: assume zero knowledge, define the topic in one sentence
H2: What is [Topic]?
H2: Key terms you need to know
H2: Getting started: your first [Task]
H2: Beginner mistakes that waste time
H2: Where to go next
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: encouragement + one resource link

### Template 6 — Advanced How-To
H1: Advanced [Task]: [Techniques] the Pros Use
Intro: state the prerequisite knowledge clearly
H2: What you should already know
H2: [Advanced technique 1]
H2: [Advanced technique 2]
H2: Optimizing for [Outcome]
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: next-level resources

### Template 7 — How-To Without [Thing]
H1: How to [Task] Without [Costly Tool / Skill]
Intro: the constraint is the hook — lean into it
H2: What you can skip (and why)
H2: The free/manual method, step by step
H2: Limitations of this approach
H2: When to upgrade to the paid way
H2: Frequently asked questions
Conclusion: honest trade-off summary
