30. Advice — How should a beginner practice [skill] in their first month?
31. Perspective — How has [field] changed since you started, and where is it going?
32. Perspective — What trends in [field] excite you, and which ones worry you?
33. Perspective — How do you think [topic] will look ten years from now?
34. Perspective — What do outsiders usually misunderstand about your work?
35. Perspective — If you could work on any project in the world, what would it be?
36. Personal — What are you reading, watching, or listening to right now?
37. Personal — How do you recharge when work gets intense?
38. Personal — What is something you are proud of that has nothing to do with work?
39. Personal — Who has influenced your thinking more than anyone else?
40. Personal — What does a perfect day off look like for you?
