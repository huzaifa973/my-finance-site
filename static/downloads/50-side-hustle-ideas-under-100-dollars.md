## Service-based (14)

1. Dog walking — pet owners pay a premium for someone trustworthy; startup ~$30 for leashes, waste bags, and flyers.
2. House cleaning — recurring weekly clients mean steady income; startup ~$40 for quality supplies and a caddy.
3. Lawn mowing and yard cleanup — every neighborhood has overgrown lawns; startup ~$60 for a used mower or borrow one first.
4. Errand running for busy neighbors — groceries, post office, pharmacy; startup ~$10 for transport and a phone.
5. Babysitting — parents always need trusted sitters; startup ~$20 for a first-aid course certificate and flyers.
6. Senior companion visits — conversation, light chores, and company; startup $0, background check builds trust.
7. Mobile car detailing — drive to the customer with your kit; startup ~$70 for soap, buckets, towels, and a vacuum.
8. Pet sitting in your home — owners prefer homes over kennels; startup ~$20 for spare bowls, bedding, and treats.
9. Laundry and ironing service — busy professionals hate ironing; startup ~$25 for detergent, hangers, and bags.
10. Window cleaning — homes and small shops, visible results; startup ~$35 for a squeegee, bucket, and extension pole.
11. Gutter cleaning — seasonal, high demand, quick jobs; startup ~$40 for a ladder stabilizer and gloves.
12. Moving help — loading, unloading, and furniture assembly; startup $0 if you have a vehicle, ~$30 for straps and a dolly rental.
13. Holiday light hanging and decorating — premium seasonal rates; startup ~$50 for clips, extension cords, and a ladder.
14. Odd-job handyman — small fixes most people avoid; startup ~$60 for a basic toolbox set.
