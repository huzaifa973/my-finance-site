## Quick rules for every headline

- Keep it under 60 characters when possible so it never truncates in search results.
- One promise per headline: a specific reader, a specific benefit.
- Numbers outperform vague words — "17 ways" beats "several ways."
- Match the article: never promise what the post doesn't deliver.
- Write 5-10 options and pick the strongest; the first draft is rarely the winner.
