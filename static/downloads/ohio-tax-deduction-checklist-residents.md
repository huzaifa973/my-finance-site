# Ohio Tax Deduction Checklist for Residents

Ohio's tax return has more moving parts than most residents expect: a state income tax, a school district tax in many districts, and a stack of credits and deductions that do not appear on the federal return. This twenty-point checklist walks Ohio residents through the items worth checking before filing — retirement credits, the 529 and ABLE deductions, business income rules, and property tax relief — so nothing valuable is missed. It is written for Ohio residents filing their own returns, and for anyone helping family members file in the state.

## Resource

1. Ohio state income tax — check the current brackets and your taxable income
2. Ohio school district income tax — many districts levy one; confirm yours
3. Ohio retirement income credit — up to $200 if you qualify
4. Senior citizen credit — $50 per return for qualifying seniors
5. Earned income credit — Ohio matches 30% of the federal credit
6. Child and dependent care credit — check Ohio's version of the federal credit
7. 529 college savings deduction — up to $4,000 per beneficiary per year
8. Ohio ABLE account contributions — deductible up to $4,000 per beneficiary
9. Medical expense deduction — Ohio allows expenses above 7.5% of AGI
10. Business income deduction — up to $250,000 of business income
11. Small business investor income deduction — check the current percentage
12. Ohio use tax — report out-of-state online purchases
13. Property tax homestead exemption — seniors and disabled homeowners
14. Owner-occupancy credit — 2.5% reduction on qualifying homes
15. Nonbusiness credit — the $20 per exemption credit
16. Joint filing credit — available when both spouses have income
17. Lump-sum retirement credit — for qualifying lump-sum distributions
18. Disability and survivorship benefits — check Ohio's exemptions
19. Military pay exclusions — active-duty and reserve rules differ
20. Keep W-2s, 1099s, and school district codes filed together
