# 50 PDF Metadata Title Templates

The title baked into a PDF's metadata is what shows up in search results, document managers, and browser tabs — and most PDFs still carry the filename the author forgot to change, like 'final_v7.pdf'. These fifty templates give every document type a clean, searchable title format: report, guide, manual, proposal, invoice, contract, whitepaper, and more. They are made for office workers, freelancers, publishers, and anyone who sends PDFs to clients or publishes them online.

## How to use

1. Pick the items that fit your situation and ignore the rest — a template pack works best when you use ten items well, not all of them once.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder ([Business Name], [topic], [Name]) with your own details before publishing or sharing.
3. Save your customized version in one place so you can reuse it next time without starting over.
4. Review the result once in its real context — a listing, a page, or a reply — before moving on.
