# 50 Local SEO Citation Templates

Local search runs on consistency. When your business name, address, and phone number match exactly everywhere — Google, Yelp, directories, and niche listings — search engines trust your listing more, and customers find you instead of a competitor. These fifty citation templates give you the exact formats to use, so you never have to guess how to write your details on a new directory again. They are built for small business owners, local marketers, and anyone managing listings for a shop, clinic, restaurant, or service business.

## How to use

1. Pick the items that fit your situation and ignore the rest — a template pack works best when you use ten items well, not all of them once.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder ([Business Name], [topic], [Name]) with your own details before publishing or sharing.
3. Save your customized version in one place so you can reuse it next time without starting over.
4. Review the result once in its real context — a listing, a page, or a reply — before moving on.
