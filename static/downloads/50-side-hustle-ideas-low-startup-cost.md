50 Side Hustle Ideas With Low Startup Costs — free resource from DollarWise

1. Freelance proofreading — start with a laptop and a style guide; under $50 for a basic course.
2. Freelance writing — pitch articles and blog posts; zero startup cost beyond a portfolio of samples.
3. Virtual assistance — manage inboxes and schedules for busy founders; needs only a computer.
4. Social media management — run accounts for small businesses; free tools cover scheduling.
5. Resume writing — help job seekers stand out; a few sample resumes are your portfolio.
6. Transcription — turn audio into text; a decent headset is your main purchase.
7. Translation — if you speak two languages, offer document and subtitle translation.
8. Bookkeeping for small businesses — a spreadsheet and basic accounting knowledge to start.
9. Online tutoring — teach a school subject or language over video calls.
10. Music lessons online — teach an instrument you already play, from your own home.
11. Freelance graphic design — start with free design tools and a small portfolio.
12. Photo editing — retouch photos for photographers and sellers; software trials keep costs low.
13. Video editing — edit short-form videos for creators; free editors are enough to begin.
14. Voiceover work — a USB microphone under $50 opens the door to narration gigs.
15. Sell printables on marketplaces — planners, checklists, and worksheets made with free tools.
16. Sell digital templates — resume, budget, and social media templates with no inventory.
17. Print on demand — designs on shirts and mugs with no stock to buy upfront.
18. Flip thrift finds — resell secondhand clothes, books, and electronics from charity shops.
19. Sell handmade crafts — candles, jewelry, or soap, starting with small material batches.
20. Sell stock photos — upload photos you already take to stock libraries for passive income.
21. Sell used textbooks — buy low at semester end and resell to next year's students.
22. Refurbish furniture — sand, paint, and resell pieces found cheaply or free.
23. Create an online course — teach one skill you know well, recorded on your phone.
24. Start a niche newsletter — build an audience around one topic and add sponsors later.
25. Write and sell an ebook — package your knowledge into a short, focused guide.
26. Start a faceless YouTube channel — screen recordings and voiceovers, no camera needed.
27. Sell presets and filters — photo and video presets for creators who want your look.
28. Build a paid community — a small, focused group around a shared interest or goal.
29. Dog walking — start in your neighborhood with flyers and a free listing.
30. Pet sitting — care for pets while owners travel; trust and reviews are your marketing.
31. House cleaning — basic supplies under $50 and clients found through local groups.
32. Gardening help — mowing, weeding, and planting for neighbors with basic tools.
33. Handyman tasks — small fixes and assembly jobs if you are practical and careful.
34. Moving help — offer an extra pair of hands and a vehicle for local moves.

36. Errand running — pickups, returns, and queues for people short on time.

38. Tutoring in person — help local students after school, advertised through schools.
