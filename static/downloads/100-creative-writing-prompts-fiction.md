100 Creative Writing Prompts for Fiction — free resource from DollarWise

1. A lighthouse keeper discovers a letter addressed to her that was written fifty years before she was born.
2. A street musician's violin starts playing a tune no one taught him, and the city stops to listen.
3. A retired detective receives a case file about a crime she already solved — but the details are all wrong.
4. A baker finds a recipe in her grandmother's cookbook that, when baked, makes people remember a forgotten day.
5. A taxi driver picks up a passenger who knows exactly what he is thinking before he says it.
6. A librarian notices one book that is never on the shelf, no matter how many times it is returned.
7. A young astronaut wakes from cryosleep to find the ship's crew gone and a garden growing in the cargo bay.
8. A cartographer maps a coastline that changes shape every time she looks away from it.
9. A chess prodigy loses her first game to a stranger who plays moves from a century-old match.
10. A gardener plants seeds that grow into flowers matching the moods of whoever walks past them.
11. A night-shift nurse hears a patient humming a lullaby she sang to her own child years ago.
12. A blacksmith forges a sword that refuses to be sharpened, no matter how many times it is ground.
13. A schoolteacher finds her students' drawings predicting tomorrow's weather with perfect accuracy.
14. A fisherman pulls up a bottle containing a map of a town that does not exist on any chart.
15. A clockmaker repairs a watch that runs backward, and its owner ages in reverse each day it ticks.
16. A young chef inherits a food truck with a menu written in a language nobody can read — yet customers order perfectly.
17. A postman delivers a letter to an empty house every day for a year, and one day someone answers the door.
18. A seamstress sews a coat that keeps its wearer warm in any weather, but only if they are telling the truth.
19. A mountain guide leads a group up a peak that appears on no map, and the trail vanishes behind them.
20. A photographer develops a roll of film showing people who were never in front of the camera.
21. An inventor builds a machine that translates birdsong, and the birds have urgent news.
22. A sailor hears her name called from beneath the waves on a perfectly calm night.
23. A child befriends the ghost of a dog that only appears when it rains.
24. A wedding planner discovers that two of her clients are the same couple, married in two different lives.
25. A firefighter saves a house from a blaze and finds a room inside that was never in the floor plan.
26. A jeweler cuts a stone that glows faintly whenever its owner is about to make a life-changing choice.
27. A train conductor notices one passenger who boards every night but never gets off at any station.
28. A beekeeper's hives hum a melody that matches the song her late mother used to sing.
29. A young lawyer inherits her grandfather's briefcase, which contains cases that have not happened yet.
30. A tailor in a small town makes suits that fit people perfectly on the first try, without ever measuring them.
31. A river guide finds a message carved into a rock that only appears when the water level drops.
32. A museum curator opens a crate labeled with her own name, dated a hundred years ago.
33. A pizza delivery rider keeps getting orders from an address that was demolished last year.
34. A shepherd counts his flock each night and always finds one extra sheep that was not there at dawn.
35. A barista writes customers' names on cups and discovers one name predicts who will return the next day.
36. A violin maker finishes an instrument that plays itself softly whenever its owner is sad.
37. A snowplow driver clears a road that leads to a village nobody in town remembers.
38. A young astronomer spots a star that blinks in a pattern spelling her initials in Morse code.
39. A locksmith opens a door that has no keyhole and finds a room full of doors just like it.
40. A florist arranges a bouquet that makes whoever receives it dream of the same garden that night.
41. A marathon runner trains on a route where every mile marker shows a different year of her life.
42. A chef's new dish makes diners recall a meal from their childhood they had completely forgotten.

61. A street vendor sells umbrellas that only open when the buyer truly needs shelter.
62. A cartographer's apprentice draws a map of his hometown and finds a street he has never walked.
63. A lighthouse keeper's logbook contains entries in handwriting that is not hers, describing storms yet to come.
64. A pastry chef bakes a cake that tastes different to each person who eats it, matching their favorite memory.
65. A train passenger wakes in a carriage that is empty except for a ticket with her name and tomorrow's date.
66. A young botanist discovers a plant that blooms only when someone nearby tells a secret aloud.
67. A taxi driver realizes every passenger he picks up on Fridays is heading to the same address.
