# Podcast and Video Repurposing Checklist — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

One episode can become a week of content if you plan the repurposing before you publish. Work through this checklist for each episode, starting with your best-performing ones.

## Plan

1. Pick one strong episode or video to repurpose — start with your best-performing content.
2. Watch or listen once and mark timestamps for the best moments, quotes, and stories.
3. Write down the three to five key takeaways the episode actually delivers.

## Short-form clips

4. Cut 3 short vertical clips (30-90 seconds) for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok.
5. Add captions to every clip — most viewers watch with sound off.
6. Turn one strong quote into a square image post for social feeds.
7. Write a short thread or carousel from the episode's main points.
8. Create a blog post or show-notes page summarizing the episode with links.

## Written content

9. Extract the audio as a standalone podcast episode or bonus clip.
10. Make a audiogram: a short audio clip with a waveform animation.
11. Turn the episode's steps or tips into a checklist or cheat sheet.
12. Record a 60-second teaser that points viewers to the full episode.
13. Create a thumbnail or cover image for each clip, not just the full video.
14. Write a newsletter issue around the episode with one clear takeaway.

## Visual and bonus content

15. Turn listener questions from the episode into a Q&A post.
16. Make a behind-the-scenes post about how the episode was recorded.
17. Cut a blooper or outtake reel if the recording has fun moments.
18. Create a playlist grouping related episodes by topic.
19. Repurpose the transcript into a downloadable PDF guide.
20. Turn statistics or facts from the episode into an infographic.

## Publish and measure

21. Post clips on a staggered schedule: one per day, not all at once.
22. Link every clip back to the full episode with a clear call to action.
23. Repurpose the best-performing clip again in a new format after a month.
24. Track which formats get the most views and double down on those.
25. Ask your audience which clip they liked best and make more like it.
26. Save all repurposed files in one folder per episode for easy reuse.
27. Update old show notes with links to the new clips and posts.
28. Revisit top episodes every quarter and repurpose them for new audiences.
