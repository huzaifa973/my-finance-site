# 50 Fill-in-the-Blank Podcast Intro Script Templates

Free resource from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz). Fill in every [BRACKET] with your own details, read the script aloud once, and record. Aim for 30 to 60 seconds per intro.

## Interview shows (1-10)

1. Welcome to [SHOW NAME], the show where [TAGLINE]. I am your host, [YOUR NAME], and today I am joined by [GUEST NAME], [GUEST TITLE]. We are diving into [TOPIC] and what it means for [AUDIENCE]. Let us get started.

2. You are listening to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME], and in this episode I sit down with [GUEST NAME] to talk about [TOPIC]. If you have ever wondered [AUDIENCE QUESTION], this conversation is for you.

3. Welcome back to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. My guest today is [GUEST NAME], [GUEST CREDENTIAL]. We talk about [TOPIC], [GUEST ACHIEVEMENT], and the lessons you can steal for your own [FIELD]. Here we go.

4. This is [SHOW NAME] with [YOUR NAME]. Today I am speaking with [GUEST NAME] about [TOPIC]. By the end of our conversation, you will understand [KEY INSIGHT]. Let us welcome [GUEST FIRST NAME].

5. Hey everyone, [YOUR NAME] here with another episode of [SHOW NAME]. Today: a conversation with [GUEST NAME], [GUEST TITLE], on [TOPIC]. We cover [SUBTOPIC ONE], [SUBTOPIC TWO], and the story behind [INTERESTING DETAIL].

6. Welcome to [SHOW NAME], where I interview [GUEST TYPE] about [THEME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. My guest is [GUEST NAME], and today we dig into [TOPIC] — including the moment everything changed for them.

7. You are listening to [SHOW NAME]. I am your host [YOUR NAME]. In this episode, [GUEST NAME], [GUEST TITLE], shares [WHAT THEY SHARE] and answers the question every [AUDIENCE] asks: [AUDIENCE QUESTION].

8. This is [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME], and today I welcome [GUEST NAME] to the show. [HE OR SHE] is [GUEST CREDENTIAL], and we are talking [TOPIC] — the wins, the mistakes, and what [HE OR SHE] would do differently.

9. Welcome to a new episode of [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. [GUEST NAME] joins me to discuss [TOPIC], and trust me, the story about [TEASER DETAIL] alone is worth the listen.

10. Hi, I am [YOUR NAME], host of [SHOW NAME]. My guest today needs little introduction: [GUEST NAME], [GUEST TITLE]. We go deep on [TOPIC], and [GUEST FIRST NAME] does not hold back. Enjoy.

## Solo shows (11-20)

11. Hey everyone, welcome back to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME], and today we are talking about [TOPIC]. By the end of this episode, you will know [KEY TAKEAWAY]. Let us dive in.

12. This is [SHOW NAME], the podcast that helps [AUDIENCE] achieve [DESIRED OUTCOME]. I am your host [YOUR NAME]. Today: [EPISODE TOPIC], and the [NUMBER] lessons I wish I had learned sooner.

13. Welcome to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. In this episode I am sharing [WHAT YOU SHARE] about [TOPIC] — including the mistake that cost me [COST] and what I do instead now.

14. You are listening to [SHOW NAME] with [YOUR NAME]. Today is a solo episode, and I want to talk honestly about [TOPIC]. If you are [AUDIENCE SITUATION], this one is for you.

15. Hey, [YOUR NAME] here, and welcome to [SHOW NAME]. Quick episode today: [NUMBER] thoughts on [TOPIC] that changed how I [VERB]. Let us get into it.

16. This is [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME], and today we are doing a deep dive on [TOPIC]. I will walk you through [STEP SUMMARY] so you can [DESIRED OUTCOME] by the end of the week.

17. Welcome back to [SHOW NAME], I am [YOUR NAME]. Last week we covered [PREVIOUS TOPIC]; today we build on that with [TOPIC]. Here is what you need to know.

18. Hi everyone, this is [YOUR NAME] with [SHOW NAME]. Today I answer the question I get asked most: [AUDIENCE QUESTION]. My honest answer, in [LENGTH], starts now.

19. You are listening to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. In this episode: why [COMMON BELIEF] is wrong about [TOPIC], and what actually works instead.

20. Welcome to [SHOW NAME] — I am [YOUR NAME]. Grab [BEVERAGE OR SETTING], because today we are unpacking [TOPIC] piece by piece until it finally clicks.

## True crime (21-28)

21. Welcome to [SHOW NAME]. On [DATE], in [LOCATION], [BRIEF CASE HOOK]. I am [YOUR NAME], and this is the story of [CASE NAME]. Listener discretion is advised.

22. This is [SHOW NAME], where we investigate [CASE TYPE] and the questions that were never answered. I am [YOUR NAME]. Today we reopen the file on [CASE NAME].

23. In [YEAR], [VICTIM DESCRIPTION] vanished from [LOCATION]. What happened next baffled investigators for [TIME PERIOD]. I am [YOUR NAME], and this is [SHOW NAME].

24. Welcome to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. Tonight: the [CASE NAME] case — [ONE-LINE HOOK]. We will follow the evidence, the suspects, and the detail everyone missed.

25. This is [SHOW NAME]. [DATE]. [LOCATION]. A [EVENT DESCRIPTION] that would haunt [COMMUNITY] for decades. I am [YOUR NAME] — let us begin.

26. On [SHOW NAME], we do not just retell crimes — we examine them. I am [YOUR NAME]. Today: [CASE NAME], and the [NUMBER] pieces of evidence that changed everything.

27. Welcome to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. [CASE HOOK IN ONE SENTENCE]. Over the next [LENGTH], we will trace what happened, who was involved, and what remains unsolved.

28. This is [SHOW NAME] with [YOUR NAME]. Every case has a moment where it turns. For [CASE NAME], that moment came on [DATE], when [TURNING POINT]. This is that story.

41. You are listening to [SHOW NAME]. I am [YOUR NAME]. Fair warning: this episode contains [FUNNY WARNING] and at least [NUMBER] bad impressions. Our topic: [TOPIC].
