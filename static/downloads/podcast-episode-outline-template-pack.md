# Podcast Episode Outline Template Pack (5 formats) — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

Five one-page, fill-in-the-blank episode outlines. Each follows the same skeleton: a one-sentence goal, timed segments, and a call to action.

---

## 1. Interview episode outline

Episode title: [Episode Title]
Guest: [Guest Name], [Credentials/Role]
Episode goal (one sentence): [What the listener will learn or gain]

SEGMENT 1 — Cold open hook (0:00-0:45)
[Your strongest 30-second clip or a bold claim from the guest]

SEGMENT 2 — Intro + guest bio (0:45-3:00)
[Welcome listeners, episode topic, why it matters, guest introduction]

SEGMENT 3 — Backstory questions (3:00-12:00)

1. [How did you get started in ___?]
2. [What was the hardest part of the early days?]

SEGMENT 4 — Core expertise questions (12:00-28:00)

1. [What is the biggest misconception about ___?]
2. [Walk us through your process for ___]
3. [What would you tell someone starting today?]

SEGMENT 5 — Rapid-fire round (28:00-32:00)
[3-5 quick questions: favorite tool, best advice, one book, etc.]

SEGMENT 6 — Wrap-up + CTA (32:00-35:00)
[Where to find the guest, their offer, subscribe/review CTA, sign-off]
