# 50 Podcast Chapter Title Templates

Chapter markers are the table of contents for a podcast episode — they let listeners jump to the part they care about, and they show up in players that support chapters. But most episodes ship with chapters like 'Part 1' and 'Part 2', which help nobody. These fifty templates give every section of an episode a clear, skimmable title, across five common topics. They are written for podcasters, audio editors, and show producers adding chapters to new or back-catalog episodes.

## How to use

1. Pick the items that fit your situation and ignore the rest — a template pack works best when you use ten items well, not all of them once.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder ([Business Name], [topic], [Name]) with your own details before publishing or sharing.
3. Save your customized version in one place so you can reuse it next time without starting over.
4. Review the result once in its real context — a listing, a page, or a reply — before moving on.
