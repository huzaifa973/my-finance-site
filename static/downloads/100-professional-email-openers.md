## Cold outreach

1. I came across your work on [topic] and had to reach out.
2. Your recent post about [topic] really stood out to me.
3. I have been following [company] for a while, and I admire how you approach [topic].
4. A mutual connection suggested I get in touch about [topic].
5. I noticed [company] is growing fast in [topic] — congratulations.
6. Your approach to [topic] is refreshingly different, and I would love to learn more.
7. I help teams like yours with [topic], and I had an idea worth two minutes of your time.
8. I am reaching out because [company]'s work in [topic] caught my eye.
9. After reading your article on [topic], I wanted to introduce myself.
10. I believe there may be a simple way to help [company] with [topic].

## Follow-ups

11. Just floating this back to the top of your inbox.
12. I wanted to follow up on my note about [topic] from last week.
13. Checking in — did you get a chance to review [topic]?
14. No rush at all, but I wanted to make sure this did not get buried.
15. Following up once more on [topic] before I close the loop.
16. I know inboxes get busy, so here is a quick recap of [topic].
17. Just a gentle nudge on [topic] — happy to work around your schedule.
18. Circling back on [topic] with one new detail that may help.
19. I wanted to check whether [topic] is still on your radar.
20. Quick follow-up: is now a better time to discuss [topic]?
