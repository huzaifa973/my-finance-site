# 50 Meeting Agenda Templates for Teams — free download from DollarWise (dollarwise.xyz)

The templates follow one rule: every item ends with either a decision, an owner, or a next step. Items without one get cut. Timings are suggestions — shrink them for small teams, stretch them for big ones — but keep the structure, because structure is what turns a chat into a meeting.
