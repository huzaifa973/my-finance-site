## Seed keyword ideas

1. Act as an SEO strategist. Generate 30 seed keyword ideas for the niche [NICHE]. Include head terms, topic variations, and common synonyms. Format as a numbered list grouped by theme.
2. Brainstorm 20 keyword themes for a website about [NICHE] aimed at [TARGET AUDIENCE]. For each theme, give 3 example keywords at different stages of awareness: beginner, intermediate, expert.
3. Give me 25 alternative ways people might phrase the search for [SEED KEYWORD], including slang, synonyms, plural/singular variants, and natural-language phrasing.
4. What are 20 subtopics within [BROAD TOPIC] that deserve their own dedicated articles? List each as a potential primary keyword.
5. Generate keyword ideas for [NICHE] from the perspective of a [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / EXPERT] audience. What would each level search for differently?
6. List 30 two-to-four-word keyword phrases combining [CORE TERM] with common modifiers like best, how, vs, for, cheap, near me, guide, review, tips, examples.
7. I run a [TYPE OF SITE] about [NICHE]. Generate 25 keyword ideas focused on problems my audience is trying to solve, phrased as they would type them into Google.
8. What seasonal or trending keyword opportunities exist in [NICHE] for [CURRENT MONTH/YEAR]? List 15 with a note on when each peaks.
9. Generate 20 keyword ideas targeting [LOCAL AREA] combined with [SERVICE OR TOPIC], including "near me" variants and neighborhood-level phrasing.
10. Expand the seed keyword [SEED KEYWORD] into 30 ideas across these angles: definitions, how-tos, lists, comparisons, costs, mistakes, tools, and expert opinions.
