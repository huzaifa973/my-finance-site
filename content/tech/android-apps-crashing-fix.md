Few things are more annoying than an app that closes the second you open it. App crashes on Android usually come down to a corrupted cache, an outdated app, or a phone running low on storage. Work through these eight fixes in order — most people solve it by step four.

## 8 fixes that work

**1. Force stop the app and reopen it.** A stuck process is the most common cause. Go to Settings > Apps, tap the crashing app, then tap Force stop. Open the app again and see if it behaves.

**2. Restart your phone.** It sounds too simple, but a restart clears temporary glitches in the system. Hold the power button, tap Restart, and try the app again once the phone boots up.

**3. Clear the app's cache.** Corrupted temporary files are a classic crash trigger, and clearing them deletes no personal data. Go to Settings > Apps, tap the app, tap Storage & cache, then tap Clear cache.

**4. Update the app.** Developers fix crash bugs in updates all the time. Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, go to Manage apps & device > Updates available, and update the crashing app — or update everything while you're there.

**5. Update Android itself.** An old system version can clash with newer apps. Go to Settings > System > System update and install anything waiting. These updates often include stability fixes.

**6. Clear the app's data (last resort before reinstalling).** If the cache clear didn't help, corrupted app data might be the issue. Go to Settings > Apps > [app] > Storage & cache and tap Clear storage. Warning: this logs you out and deletes the app's saved data, so only do this if you can sign back in.

**7. Uninstall and reinstall the app.** This gives you a completely fresh copy. Long-press the app icon, tap Uninstall, then reinstall it from the Play Store. Make sure your data is backed up or synced to an account first.

**8. Free up storage space.** Android gets unstable when storage drops below about 10% free. Go to Settings > Storage to see what's eating space. Delete old downloads, clear out duplicate photos, or move large videos to cloud storage, then try the app again.

## The bottom line

Nearly every app crash is fixed by force-stopping, clearing the cache, or updating the app — those three cover the vast majority of cases. If one specific app still crashes after all eight steps on a phone with free space and current software, the bug is on the developer's side, so report it through the Play Store listing and wait for a fix.
