## Contact DollarWise

Have a question about a guide, found a mistake, or want to suggest a tool we should build next? We'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** hello@dollarwise.xyz

We read every message and aim to reply within a few days.

### What to contact us about

- **Corrections** — spotted an error in a guide or a bug in a tool? Tell us and we'll fix it.
- **Tool requests** — tell us what free tool would make your life easier; reader requests shape what we build.
- **Feedback** — what's working, what's confusing, what we should cover next.

### Please note

DollarWise publishes educational content only — we can't give personalized financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. For decisions about your money, please speak with a licensed professional.
