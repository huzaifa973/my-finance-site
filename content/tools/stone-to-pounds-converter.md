## About stone and pounds

The stone is a British unit equal to 14 pounds, or 6.35 kilograms. It is still widely used in the UK and Ireland for stating a person's body weight.

The pound equals 16 ounces, or exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. Pounds are the standard US unit for body weight, gym plates and grocery produce.
