## About centimeters and kilometers

A centimeter is one hundredth of a meter, about the width of an average fingernail. Centimeters are the standard unit for body height, furniture and school rulers across most of the world.

A kilometer is 1,000 meters — roughly a 10 to 12 minute walk. Kilometers, and kilometers per hour, are used for road distances and speed limits in almost every country except the US and UK.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many kilometers are in a centimeter?**
1 kilometer = 100,000 centimeters, so 1 centimeter is only about 1e-05 kilometers. For small units like this, the table below is the quickest way to read off a value.

**How do I convert centimeters to kilometers manually?**
Divide the centimeters value by 100,000. For example, 30,000 cm ÷ 100,000 = 0.3 km.
