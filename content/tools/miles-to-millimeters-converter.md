## About miles and millimeters

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.

The millimeter is one thousandth of a meter — the smallest length unit most people meet day to day. Millimeters are used for rainfall, drill bits, engineering tolerances and phone thickness, anywhere centimeters are too coarse.
