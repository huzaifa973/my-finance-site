## About ounces and kilograms

The ounce is a US customary unit equal to one sixteenth of a pound, or 28.35 grams. Ounces are used in American recipes, packaged food weights and postal services.

The kilogram is the base unit of mass in the metric system and equals about 2.2 pounds. Body weight, luggage and groceries are measured in kilograms in most of the world.
