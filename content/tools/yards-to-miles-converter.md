## About yards and miles

The yard equals 3 feet (36 inches), or exactly 0.9144 meters. Yards show up in American football fields, fabric sold by the yard and landscaping measurements.

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.
