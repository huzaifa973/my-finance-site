- **Open-source friendly:** OGG Vorbis is patent-free — perfect for projects that avoid proprietary codecs.
- **Web audio:** OGG is a go-to format for web players and HTML5 audio.
- **Game audio:** many game engines and mods prefer OGG for soundtracks and effects.
- **Quality per byte:** Vorbis beats MP3 at the same bitrate — great for podcasts and music clips from old phone videos.
- **Linux workflows:** the default audio format across most Linux desktops and tools.
