## About centimeters and miles

A centimeter is one hundredth of a meter, about the width of an average fingernail. Centimeters are the standard unit for body height, furniture and school rulers across most of the world.

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.
