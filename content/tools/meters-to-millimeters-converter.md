## About meters and millimeters

The meter is the base unit of length in the metric system, defined by the distance light travels in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second. Meters are used worldwide for rooms, buildings, running tracks and swimming pools.

The millimeter is one thousandth of a meter — the smallest length unit most people meet day to day. Millimeters are used for rainfall, drill bits, engineering tolerances and phone thickness, anywhere centimeters are too coarse.
