## How to use it

1. **Add a clip** — a riff, a mantra, a rain loop, anything.
2. **Choose how many repeats** — the total length is shown by your file's duration × repeats.
3. **Process & download** — one long seamless track.

## About this tool

Looping repeats the audio stream end-to-end. It is how musicians practice over a backing riff, how meditation tracks run for an hour from a 5-minute recording, and how game and video editors build long ambience beds from short samples.

For a truly seamless loop, the clip's end should flow into its beginning — the Audio Trimmer and Fade Editor can help you prepare one.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a gap between repeats?**
No — repeats are joined sample-accurately with no silence added.

**How long can the result be?**
Very long files take more memory in your browser. For clips under a minute, even 10× is fine.

**Are my files uploaded?**
No — processing is local, in your browser.
