## About kilograms and grams

The kilogram is the base unit of mass in the metric system and equals about 2.2 pounds. Body weight, luggage and groceries are measured in kilograms in most of the world.

The gram is the everyday metric unit for small weights — a paperclip weighs about 1 gram. Grams are used in cooking, nutrition labels, jewelry and science all over the world.
