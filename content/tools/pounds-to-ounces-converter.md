## About pounds and ounces

The pound equals 16 ounces, or exactly 0.45359237 kilograms. Pounds are the standard US unit for body weight, gym plates and grocery produce.

The ounce is a US customary unit equal to one sixteenth of a pound, or 28.35 grams. Ounces are used in American recipes, packaged food weights and postal services.
