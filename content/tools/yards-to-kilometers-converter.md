## About yards and kilometers

The yard equals 3 feet (36 inches), or exactly 0.9144 meters. Yards show up in American football fields, fabric sold by the yard and landscaping measurements.

A kilometer is 1,000 meters — roughly a 10 to 12 minute walk. Kilometers, and kilometers per hour, are used for road distances and speed limits in almost every country except the US and UK.
