## About centimeters and inches

A centimeter is one hundredth of a meter, about the width of an average fingernail. Centimeters are the standard unit for body height, furniture and school rulers across most of the world.

The inch is an imperial and US customary unit equal to exactly 2.54 centimeters. Inches are used in the United States for screen sizes, paper, tools, rainfall and body measurements.
