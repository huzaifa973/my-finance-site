## About stone and ounces

The stone is a British unit equal to 14 pounds, or 6.35 kilograms. It is still widely used in the UK and Ireland for stating a person's body weight.

The ounce is a US customary unit equal to one sixteenth of a pound, or 28.35 grams. Ounces are used in American recipes, packaged food weights and postal services.
