## About this tool

The boomerang formula is simple and addictive: the second half is the first half reversed, so the motion lands exactly where it started and loops forever with no jump cut. Actions with a clear peak — a jump's height, a splash, a hair flip — boomerang best.

Film tips: hold the camera still, keep the action inside 2 seconds, and leave a beat at the peak. Trim the clip first with the Video Trimmer for the tightest loop.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is there no sound?**
Boomerangs are silent by design — the reversed audio would sound like gibberish, and Instagram's original format is silent too.

**How long should the clip be?**
1–5 seconds. The output is twice the input (forward + back), and reversing very long clips uses a lot of memory.

**Are my videos uploaded?**
No — the boomerang is made in your browser.
