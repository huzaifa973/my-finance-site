## About tons (us) and stone

This converter uses the US (short) ton of exactly 2,000 pounds, or 907.18 kilograms. Note that the metric tonne is slightly larger at exactly 1,000 kg — check which one your scale or document means.

The stone is a British unit equal to 14 pounds, or 6.35 kilograms. It is still widely used in the UK and Ireland for stating a person's body weight.
