## About tons (us) and kilograms

This converter uses the US (short) ton of exactly 2,000 pounds, or 907.18 kilograms. Note that the metric tonne is slightly larger at exactly 1,000 kg — check which one your scale or document means.

The kilogram is the base unit of mass in the metric system and equals about 2.2 pounds. Body weight, luggage and groceries are measured in kilograms in most of the world.
