## About JPG and ICO

**JPG** is the world's most common photo format — compact lossy files that work everywhere. **ICO** is the Windows icon format used for favicons, shortcuts and app icons. Converting from JPG to ICO is one of the most common format jobs — and with this tool it takes seconds, with no software to install.

## Why convert JPG to ICO?

- **Compatibility** — the site, app or device you use accepts ICO but not JPG.
- **Smaller uploads** — ICO files are often far smaller and faster to share.
- **Editing** — open the converted ICO file in any standard editor.
- **Publishing** — use ICO wherever a specific format is required.
