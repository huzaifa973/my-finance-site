## About inches and miles

The inch is an imperial and US customary unit equal to exactly 2.54 centimeters. Inches are used in the United States for screen sizes, paper, tools, rainfall and body measurements.

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.
