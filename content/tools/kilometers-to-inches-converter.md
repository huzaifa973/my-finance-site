## About kilometers and inches

A kilometer is 1,000 meters — roughly a 10 to 12 minute walk. Kilometers, and kilometers per hour, are used for road distances and speed limits in almost every country except the US and UK.

The inch is an imperial and US customary unit equal to exactly 2.54 centimeters. Inches are used in the United States for screen sizes, paper, tools, rainfall and body measurements.
