## About yards and feet

The yard equals 3 feet (36 inches), or exactly 0.9144 meters. Yards show up in American football fields, fabric sold by the yard and landscaping measurements.

The foot equals 12 inches, or exactly 0.3048 meters. Feet are the standard US unit for room sizes, ceiling heights, aircraft altitude and a person's height.
