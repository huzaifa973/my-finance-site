## About kilometers and miles

A kilometer is 1,000 meters — roughly a 10 to 12 minute walk. Kilometers, and kilometers per hour, are used for road distances and speed limits in almost every country except the US and UK.

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.
