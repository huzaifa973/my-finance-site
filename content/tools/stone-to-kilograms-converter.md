## About stone and kilograms

The stone is a British unit equal to 14 pounds, or 6.35 kilograms. It is still widely used in the UK and Ireland for stating a person's body weight.

The kilogram is the base unit of mass in the metric system and equals about 2.2 pounds. Body weight, luggage and groceries are measured in kilograms in most of the world.
