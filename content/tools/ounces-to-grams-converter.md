## About ounces and grams

The ounce is a US customary unit equal to one sixteenth of a pound, or 28.35 grams. Ounces are used in American recipes, packaged food weights and postal services.

The gram is the everyday metric unit for small weights — a paperclip weighs about 1 gram. Grams are used in cooking, nutrition labels, jewelry and science all over the world.
