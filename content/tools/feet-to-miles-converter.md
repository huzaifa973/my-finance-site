## About feet and miles

The foot equals 12 inches, or exactly 0.3048 meters. Feet are the standard US unit for room sizes, ceiling heights, aircraft altitude and a person's height.

The mile equals 5,280 feet, or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. Miles, and miles per hour, are used for road distance and speed in the United States and the United Kingdom.
