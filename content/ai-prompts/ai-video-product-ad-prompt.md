## Best AI tools for this

- **Google Flow (Veo)** — most photorealistic slow motion right now
- **Runway Gen-4** — great camera control for the orbital move
- **Pika** — fast iterations when you're testing product angles
- **Luma Dream Machine** — strong on liquid and fabric physics

## Make it yours

- **[PRODUCT]** — be specific: "amber glass serum bottle with gold cap" beats "skincare product"
- **Fabric** — try "white silk" + bright background for a clean beauty look
- **Splash** — swap water for "coffee splash", "ink in water", or "golden dust particles"
- **Camera** — "slow push-in" feels premium; "orbital dolly" feels epic

## Pro tips

- Generate at the highest quality setting — product ads live or die on detail.
- Keep it **under 10 seconds**; short clips have the fewest AI artifacts.
- Add your brand music and logo in CapCut/Premiere after — AI video + real editing is the winning combo.
