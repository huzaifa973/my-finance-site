## Best AI tools for this

- **Pika** — excellent at smooth, loopable motion
- **Runway Gen-4** — precise macro detail
- **Google Flow (Veo)** — most realistic textures
- **Luma Dream Machine** — great slow-motion physics

## Make it yours

- **[OBJECT]** — ideas: "glitter slime cube", "layered jelly cake", "metallic paint block", "soap carved into geometric shapes"
- **Cutting tool** — "thin wire", "hot knife", "hydraulic press", "water jet"
- **Vibe** — add "pastel colors, dreamy" for calm or "neon, high energy" for hype

## Pro tips

- Loop the clip **3–4 times** in your editor — longer watch time = more reach.
- Add a trending ASMR sound; the audio does half the work.
- Post at 9:16 vertical — this format lives on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
- Batch-generate 5 variations in one sitting and drip-feed them daily.
