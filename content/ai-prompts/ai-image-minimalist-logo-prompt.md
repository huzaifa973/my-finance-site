## Best AI tools for this

- **Midjourney** — cleanest flat vector aesthetics
- **Flux** — sharp geometric shapes
- **Recraft** — exports true SVG vectors
- **Looka / Brandmark** — AI logo makers with full brand kits

## Make it yours

- **[SUBJECT]** — "geometric fox head", "abstract letter M", "minimal mountain + sun", "origami bird"
- **Colors** — 2 max; test it in pure black too (a logo must work in one color)
- **Style words** — "line art", "badge", "monogram", "mascot" steer the direction

## Pro tips

- Generate **20+ variations**, then pick 3 — volume beats perfection here.
- Squint test: if it's readable as a tiny favicon, it's a strong mark.
- Never ship raw AI output as a final logo — refine spacing and curves.
