# Viral YouTube Thumbnail Prompt 🖼️

Top creators obsess over thumbnails because **CTR is everything**. This prompt generates the dramatic, high-contrast art style that dominates trending feeds — you add the 3-word hook text after.

## Best AI tools for this

- **Midjourney** — king of dramatic stylized art
- **Flux** — excellent faces and emotion
- **DALL-E 3 / ChatGPT images** — easy iteration in chat
- **Leonardo.ai** — built-in 16:9 and thumbnail presets

## Make it yours

- **[PERSON]** — match your channel: "shocked gamer", "confident businesswoman", "curious kid"
- **[OBJECT]** — the video's payoff: money stack, new iPhone, before/after
- **Colors** — orange+teal and red+yellow are proven CTR palettes

## Pro tips

- Faces **looking at the object** (not the camera) create a curiosity gap.
- Keep one dominant focal point — cluttered thumbnails lose.
- Always A/B test with YouTube's "Test & Compare" feature.
