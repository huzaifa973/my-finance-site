## Best AI tools for this

- **HeyGen** — best lip-sync + custom avatars
- **Synthesia** — polished corporate presenters, 140+ languages
- **Hedra** — expressive character animation from a single image
- **Google Flow (Veo)** — best for the cinematic scene itself (pair with your own voiceover)

## Make it yours

- **[AGE] / [MAN/WOMAN]** — match your target audience for instant trust
- **Setting** — "cozy home office", "modern clinic", "gym", "kitchen" for niche relevance
- **Script** — write it like you talk; 65–75 words per 30 seconds

## Pro tips

- Generate the **video muted** and add a real AI voiceover (ElevenLabs) — audio quality sells realism.
- Keep clips under 30 seconds to avoid lip-sync drift.
- A/B test two presenter styles — audience match matters more than realism.
