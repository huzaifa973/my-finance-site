# YouTube Script Prompt 🎬
Describe your video topic once — get a complete, ready-to-record 8–10 minute script with a strong hook, timestamped sections, mid-video engagement cues, and an outro, plus the full publishing package: title, five thumbnail ideas, description, and tags. This is the prompt format that turns a one-line topic into a publishable YouTube video.

Target length: 8–10 minutes (roughly 1,200–1,500 spoken words). Channel style: [FACeless voiceover / talking head / educational / storytelling]. Tone: [friendly / authoritative / casual].
Structure the script with:
1) A 15–30 second hook that states a surprising fact, asks a question, or promises a specific outcome — no slow intro.
2) A short intro where you introduce the value of the video and what the viewer will learn by the end.
3) 3–5 main points, each with a timestamp marker in [00:00] format, a clear sub-heading, and 2–3 supporting details or examples.
4) A mid-video engagement cue around the 40–50% mark asking viewers to like and subscribe, written naturally (not spammy).
5) An outro that recaps the key takeaway, includes a clear call to action (subscribe, comment, watch next video), and ends on an open loop or question.
After the script, provide:
- 5 high-click-through-rate title options (under 60 characters each, include the main keyword)
- 5 thumbnail ideas (text overlay + visual concept for each)
- A video description of 150+ words with the main keyword in the first line, plus 2–3 timestamps and relevant links
- 10–15 relevant tags, ordered by relevance
Write in a conversational, spoken-word style — short sentences, contractions, and natural pauses. Avoid jargon. No lorem ipsum.</code></pre>

## Preview — what this prompt creates
You get back a single document that reads top-to-bottom like a finished video: a punchy opening line, timestamped sections you can drop straight into your description, a natural-sounding ask for likes and subscribers in the middle, and a closing recap with a call to action. ## Make it yours
- **[YOUR TOPIC]** — be specific. "How to save $5,000 in 6 months on a $30k salary" beats "saving money tips."
- **Channel style & tone** — set these to match your existing videos so the AI writes in your voice.
- **Length** — adjust the word count target for Shorts (150–200 words) or long-form deep dives (2,000+).
## Pro tips
- Generate the script, then ask the AI to "make this sound more like [a creator you admire]" to refine the voice.
- Always add one original stat, story, or example the AI couldn't know — it's what makes your video unique.
- Record the hook separately and re-read it; the first 30 seconds decide whether anyone watches the rest.
