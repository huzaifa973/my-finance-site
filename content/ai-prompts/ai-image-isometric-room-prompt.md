## Best AI tools for this

- **Midjourney** — the cozy-isometric king
- **Flux** — crisp tiny details
- **DALL-E 3** — good at following room descriptions
- **Firefly** — safe for commercial use

## Make it yours

- **[ROOM]** — "gaming setup", "bookstore", "coffee shop", "home office", "greenhouse"
- **Palette** — "warm autumn tones", "sakura pink", "midnight blue neon", "matcha green"
- **Season** — add "snowy winter evening" or "sunny spring morning" for mood

## Pro tips

- Use the same style tail + seed for a matching **series** (great for content batches).
- These make killer YouTube backgrounds and Etsy wallpapers.
- Zoom out: generate at 16:9 for wallpapers, 1:1 for profile art.
