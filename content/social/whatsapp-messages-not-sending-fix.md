You hit send, but your WhatsApp message sits there with a single grey tick that never turns into two. That one tick means the message left your phone but never reached WhatsApp's servers — so the problem is almost always on your end. Run through these eight fixes.

## 8 quick fixes

**1. Check your internet connection.** Open your browser and load a website. If nothing loads, the problem is your connection, not WhatsApp. Toggle Wi-Fi off and on, or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to see which one works.

**2. Restart WhatsApp and your phone.** Force-close WhatsApp completely, then reopen it. If messages are still stuck, restart your phone — this clears temporary network glitches that block the app.

**3. Check if WhatsApp is down.** Sometimes the servers themselves have problems. Search "WhatsApp down" on Google or check a status site like Downdetector. If there's an outage, all you can do is wait — usually it's resolved within an hour or two.

**4. Update WhatsApp.** Old versions can develop sending bugs. Open the Play Store or App Store, search WhatsApp, and install any available update.

**5. Make sure your date and time are automatic.** WhatsApp fails to connect if your phone's clock is wrong. On Android, go to Settings > System > Date & time and turn on Set time automatically. On iPhone, go to Settings > General > Date & Time and turn on Set Automatically.

**6. Clear WhatsApp's cache (Android).** Corrupted cache files can block sending. Go to Settings > Apps > WhatsApp > Storage & cache and tap Clear cache. This deletes no chats or media.

**7. Turn off your VPN and data saver.** VPNs and aggressive data-saving modes sometimes block WhatsApp's servers. Disconnect your VPN temporarily, and on Android check Settings > Network & Internet > Data Saver is off — or at least allow WhatsApp unrestricted data usage.

**8. Reinstall WhatsApp as a last resort.** First back up your chats: in WhatsApp go to Settings > Chats > Chat backup and run a backup. Then uninstall and reinstall the app from your app store, verify your number, and restore the backup.

## One thing to rule out

If only one contact's messages stay on a single tick for days while everyone else works fine, you may have been blocked by that person — no fix on your side will change that. But if it's happening with all contacts, it's a connection or app issue, and the steps above will sort it.

## The bottom line

Single-tick problems are a connection issue nine times out of ten, so start with your internet, a restart, and the server status check. Those three take two minutes and solve the vast majority of cases without touching your chats or settings.
