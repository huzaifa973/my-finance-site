Blogger is still one of the easiest free ways to start a blog in 2026: no hosting bill, Google handles the servers, and you can be publishing in minutes. But the out-of-the-box look is plain, and your theme affects everything — how professional your blog feels, how fast it loads, and even how well it ranks on Google. Here are the seven best free Blogger themes worth using this year, starting with Google's own official options.

## 1. Contempo

Google's flagship official theme and the safest default choice. It has a clean magazine-style layout, is fully responsive, and includes a built-in theme designer so you can change colors, fonts, and layouts without touching code. If you want something that just works, start here.

## 2. Soho

Another official Google theme, designed for bold, image-heavy blogs. Soho puts big featured photos front and center with elegant typography, making it a great pick for photography, travel, food, and lifestyle blogs.

## 3. Emporio

The official theme for high-volume, news-style blogs. Its classic grid layout shows lots of posts per page, and because it is made by Google it is lightweight and fast. Ideal if you plan to publish frequently.

## 4. Notable

Google's minimalist theme for writers. Notable strips away clutter for a distraction-free reading experience and looks excellent on mobile phones, where most of your readers will find you. Perfect for personal blogs and essays.

## 5. Median UI

One of the most popular free third-party Blogger themes, with a modern card layout, dark mode toggle, and solid built-in SEO markup. It has a large community and regular updates, which matters because abandoned themes become security and compatibility risks.

## 6. Plus UI

Another community favorite that focuses on speed, with AMP-ready options and plenty of customization without coding. It is a strong pick if page speed and Core Web Vitals are a priority for you.

## 7. Magazine themes from SoraTemplates and Templateify

If you run a news or magazine-style blog, these two long-running template sites offer free newspaper layouts with ad slots built in — handy when you later add AdSense. Always download from the provider's official site, never from random re-upload links, which can hide malicious code.

## How to install a Blogger theme

1. In your Blogger dashboard, go to **Theme** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the arrow next to **Customize**, choose **Backup**, then **Download theme** — this saves your current theme in case you want to go back.
3. Click the arrow again, choose **Restore**, then **Upload**, and select the theme's `.xml` file.
4. Preview your blog on both desktop and mobile before saving, and check that your widgets and gadgets still look right.

## What to check before choosing

- **Mobile responsive.** Over half your traffic will be on phones — test every theme on a small screen.
- **Fast loading.** Run the demo through Google PageSpeed Insights; slow themes hurt rankings and ad revenue.
- **Clean SEO markup.** Proper heading structure, meta tags, and schema markup help Google understand your content.
- **Recent updates.** A theme updated in the last year is far less likely to break with Blogger changes.
- **Safe source.** Only download from official or well-known providers; free themes from shady sites sometimes contain hidden spam links.

## Recap

Start with Google's official themes — Contempo, Soho, Emporio, or Notable — for a safe, fast foundation, or level up with community favorites like Median UI and Plus UI. Back up your current theme first, then install in minutes from the Theme menu.
