## About DollarWise

DollarWise is an independent personal finance blog built for beginners. Our goal is simple: explain money in plain English so anyone can take the first step — whether that's building a first budget, saving $1,000, or starting a side hustle with no upfront cost.

### What we cover

- **Budgeting** — simple systems that work on low or irregular income
- **Saving** — realistic strategies to build an emergency fund from zero
- **Side hustles** — legit ways to earn extra income without paying to start
- **Tools** — free calculators and templates you can use right away

### What we are not

We are not financial advisors. Everything here is educational content, not personalized financial advice. For decisions about investing, taxes, or debt, consider talking to a licensed professional.

### How we keep it free

DollarWise is reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy through them — at no extra cost to you. Read our full [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
