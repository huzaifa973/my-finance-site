## How to use this pack

1. Shop the grocery list in one trip, walking each store section once.
2. Pick a prep day (Sunday works for most people) and block two hours.
3. Cook the bulk items first: grains, proteins, roasted vegetables, and soup.
4. Portion lunches and dinners into sealed containers and refrigerate immediately.
5. Keep breakfasts simple — overnight oats and egg dishes assemble in minutes.
6. Midweek, do a 30-minute refresh: cook anything fresh you need for Thursday onward.

Start with the sample week exactly as written. Once you see how little daily effort it takes, customizing it with the blank template becomes genuinely fun.

## Frequently asked questions

**What containers are best for meal prep?**
Glass containers with locking lids reheat most evenly and do not stain, but any airtight container works. The key detail is portioning single servings — one container per meal stops you from reheating a whole batch repeatedly.

**How do I keep prepped food from getting boring?**
Repeat the structure, not the flavors: keep the same protein-plus-grain-plus-vegetable formula but change the sauce or seasoning each week. A different salsa, curry paste, or herb mix makes the same ingredients taste like a new menu.
