## How to use this pack

1. Download the full pack using the button below and pick the category that matches your product.
2. Copy the prompt, replace the product description with your own item and brand colors.
3. Paste it into any AI image generator and generate two or three variations.
4. Pick the strongest shot and touch up small flaws in a free photo editor.
5. For a consistent store look, reuse the same lighting and background words across all your prompts.

One honest note: AI images show a plausible version of your product, not your exact item — labels, logos, and fine details will differ. That makes these prompts perfect for lifestyle scenes, backgrounds, mockups, and ad creative, while your hero listing shots should still show the real product. Many sellers combine both: a real photo for the main image, AI-generated scenes for the supporting gallery.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI-generated product photos replace a real photoshoot?**
For mockups, listings, and social content they work well, but the generated product will not match your exact item. For hero shots of the real product, photograph the actual item and use AI for backgrounds, lifestyle scenes, and ad creative.

**Do these prompts work with free AI image generators?**
Yes. They use plain descriptive language that works with any text-to-image tool. Add your generator's quality tags, such as photorealistic or 8k, for extra polish.

**How do I keep a consistent look across all my product shots?**
Reuse the same background, lighting, and style words in every prompt — for example "soft studio lighting, warm beige background". Consistency in the wording gives consistency in the results, which makes your store look professional.
