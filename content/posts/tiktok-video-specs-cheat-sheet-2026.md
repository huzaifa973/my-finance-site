## Frequently asked questions

**What resolution should I export vertical videos in?**

Export at 1080 x 1920 pixels with a 9:16 aspect ratio. It is the native resolution of TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and WhatsApp Status, so your video fills the screen on all four with no black bars or cropping.

**Why does WhatsApp ruin my video quality?**

WhatsApp recompresses any video over its 16 MB limit aggressively. Export at 720p with a bitrate around 5 Mbps before sending, and the file stays under the cap, which skips the worst of WhatsApp's own compression.

**How long can a YouTube Short be in 2026?**

Up to 3 minutes. YouTube raised the Shorts limit from 60 seconds in late 2024, and Instagram Reels followed with its own 3-minute limit in early 2025. TikTok still leads on length at 10 minutes.
