## What the 50 ideas cover

The **service-based** ideas (14) sell your time and reliability to people nearby — dog walking, cleaning, errand running, lawn care. These pay fastest because demand is immediate and trust-based. The **creative** ideas (12) turn skills like design, photography, or video editing into paid gigs and products. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can you really start a side hustle with under $100?**
Yes — most service and online hustles need almost nothing to start. Your costs are typically basic supplies, a domain name, or transport. The real investment is your time: expect 5-10 hours a week for the first month or two before income becomes steady.

**Which side hustle makes money fastest?**
Local services: dog walking, cleaning, lawn care, and errand running can pay within days because you are selling to neighbors who need help now. Online hustles like freelancing take longer to land the first client but scale better and have no commute.

**Do I need a business license for a side hustle?**
It depends on your location and the hustle. Simple services like dog walking or freelance writing usually need nothing to start; selling food, offering childcare, or running a market stall often requires permits. Check your city or county's small business rules before you take your first paying customer.
