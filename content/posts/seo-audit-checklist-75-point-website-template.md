An SEO audit is how you find the invisible problems holding a site back: the crawl traps, the duplicate titles, the thin pages, the toxic links. Most site owners never audit at all — they publish, wait, and wonder why traffic flatlines. This 75-point checklist changes that. It walks you through every layer of a professional audit, from server-level technical issues to off-page authority, in a logical order that surfaces the highest-impact fixes first. Agencies charge hundreds of dollars for audits built on checklists exactly like this one. Here it is, free, as a checkable template you can reuse on every site you touch.

## What's inside this pack

The checklist is organized into five sections that mirror how search engines actually evaluate your site. Technical SEO (20 checks) covers crawling, indexing, speed, and site architecture — the foundation everything else rests on. On-page SEO (20 checks) covers titles, headings, meta descriptions, images, and internal linking on individual pages. Content (15 checks) evaluates depth, freshness, duplication, and search-intent match across your library. Off-page (10 checks) covers your backlink profile, anchor text, and brand mentions. Local and mobile (10 checks) covers Google Business Profile, local citations, and the mobile experience that now drives most searches. Each check is written as a concrete pass-or-fail action, so there is no ambiguity about what to verify or fix.

## How to use this pack

1. Download the full checklist and work top to bottom — technical first, because on-page fixes cannot outrank crawl problems.
2. Mark each check as pass, fail, or not applicable, and note the affected URLs for every failure.
3. Score each failure by impact and effort, then fix the high-impact, low-effort items first for the fastest ranking wins.
4. Re-run the failed checks after fixing to confirm the issue is truly resolved, not just patched.
5. Save the completed audit with its date — comparing quarterly audits is how you prove progress and catch regressions early.

One disciplined pass through all 75 checks typically surfaces a short list of fixes that move the needle within weeks. The real power of the template is repetition: run it on a schedule and small problems never grow into traffic collapses.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a 75-point audit take?**
For a small site under 50 pages, about half a day with free tools. Larger sites take longer mostly because of crawl time, but the checklist keeps you focused so you spend time fixing rather than wondering what to check next.

**What should I fix first after an audit?**
Indexing and crawling issues first, then page speed, then on-page basics like titles and headings. Content and link problems matter enormously but take longer to fix, so clear the technical blockers before investing there.
