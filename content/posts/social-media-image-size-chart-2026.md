## Frequently asked questions

**What happens if I upload the wrong image size?**
The platform resizes or crops your image automatically to fit its frame — usually by cutting off the edges, which is exactly where text and logos tend to sit. The result looks amateur even when the design was good. Uploading at the recommended dimensions keeps every element exactly where you placed it.

**Do these sizes change often?**
Major platforms adjust dimensions every year or two, and 2026 brought small updates to several story, cover, and thumbnail formats. The aspect ratios change less often than the pixel counts. Check the sizes for your most-used platforms once a year, and any time a new upload looks unexpectedly cropped.

**What file format should I use for social images?**
JPG for photographs — it compresses well and every platform accepts it. PNG for graphics with text, logos, or transparency, where JPG artifacts would show. Keep files under each platform's upload limit, usually 8 to 30 MB, and export at the chart's dimensions rather than uploading something huge and hoping for the best.
