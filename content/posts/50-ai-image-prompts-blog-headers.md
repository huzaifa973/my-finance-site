A great blog header does two jobs: it stops the scroll and it sets the tone for the article. Stock photos are easy but they are also everywhere — readers have seen the same handshake and laptop images a hundred times. AI image generators changed that. With a well-written prompt you can create a header that matches your exact topic, brand colors, and style in under a minute, and it costs nothing.

This collection gives you 50 ready-to-use prompts organized by visual style. Each prompt is written to work across the major AI image tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Firefly) and produces header-friendly compositions: wide framing, clean negative space for titles, and no busy clutter in the middle. Copy any prompt as-is, swap in your topic keywords, and generate. Every prompt includes a copy button so you can grab it in one click.

## What's inside this prompt collection

The 50 prompts are grouped into five styles of 10. Photography-style prompts create realistic, editorial images that look like commissioned shoots. Flat lay prompts build clean top-down compositions — perfect for how-to and lifestyle articles. Abstract and gradient prompts give you modern, on-brand backgrounds that work with any topic. Illustration prompts produce vector-style and hand-drawn art for friendly, approachable blogs. Minimal and negative-space prompts leave room for your headline text, which is ideal for featured images with text overlays. Each prompt is a single line: subject, style, lighting, and composition in one paste-ready package.

## How to use this prompt collection

1. Pick the style that matches your blog's visual identity — stick to one style per series of posts for consistency.
2. Copy a prompt and replace `[topic]` (and `[brand colors]` where present) with your article's subject.
3. Generate at your theme's header aspect ratio — 16:9 (1920x1080) works for most WordPress and Hugo themes.
4. Generate 3-4 variations and pick the one with the cleanest composition and most usable negative space.
5. Compress the winner to under 200 KB, add descriptive alt text, and upload — don't skip optimization just because the image is AI-made.

The biggest beginner mistake is prompting for a subject with no composition guidance. Every prompt here ends with framing instructions (wide composition, copy space, banner format) because a beautiful square image that crops badly is useless as a header. Also, keep a short style guide: once you find a look you like, note the exact phrases that produced it and reuse them. Five posts with the same visual DNA look like a publication; five random styles look like a mood board.

## Frequently asked questions

**What size should AI-generated blog headers be?**
Generate at the aspect ratio your theme uses — 16:9 is the most common for blog headers. Request 1920x1080 or larger from the generator, then compress the result to under 200 KB before uploading, following our image optimization checklist.

**Can I use AI-generated images on my blog commercially?**
Most major AI image tools grant commercial use rights to generated images, but terms vary by platform and plan. Check your generator's terms of service, and avoid prompting for recognizable people, brand logos, or copyrighted characters.

**How do I get consistent header images across posts?**
Reuse the same style anchors across prompts — the same phrases like "flat vector illustration, muted palette, soft gradient background" plus a fixed aspect ratio. A short style guide of 2-3 signature phrases keeps a whole blog visually coherent.
