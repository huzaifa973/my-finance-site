## How to use these prompts

1. Copy a prompt from the group that matches where the image will live on your page.
2. Paste it into your AI image generator as-is, adding only your preferred aspect ratio at the end.
3. Generate two or three variants and pick the cleanest composition — flat vector styles vary between runs.
4. If the generator sneaks text into the image, regenerate rather than trying to fix it; the "no text" instruction works best on a fresh run.
5. Reuse the same palette words across posts to build a recognizable visual identity for your blog.

One more tip: generate at a larger size than you need and compress down. Vector-style art downscales beautifully and stays crisp on retina screens.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why do the prompts say "no text"?**
AI image generators still garble rendered text more often than not. Keeping text out of the prompt avoids misspelled words baked into your artwork; add headlines and labels afterward in your design tool where you control the typography.

**Can I use AI-generated illustrations commercially on my blog?**
On most major generators, yes — paid tiers typically include commercial rights. Always check your specific tool's current terms, since policies differ between providers and change over time.

**How do I keep illustrations consistent across my whole blog?**
Stick to one style phrase ("flat vector illustration") and recycle the same two or three palette descriptions across every prompt. Consistency comes from repeating the style and color language, not from the subject matter.
