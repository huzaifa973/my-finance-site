Keyword research used to mean exporting a spreadsheet of 500 keywords, sorting by volume, and guessing which ones you could rank for. AI changes the game — not by replacing keyword tools, but by doing the creative half of research in minutes: brainstorming seed ideas, uncovering the questions your audience actually asks, classifying search intent, and spotting gaps your competitors missed. The catch is that vague prompts produce vague keywords. Ask for "keywords about fitness" and you get a list you already knew. Ask with context, constraints, and the right angle, and the AI becomes a research partner that surfaces ideas you'd never have found alone.

## What's inside this prompt pack

The 50 prompts are organized into six research workflows. Seed keyword ideas (10 prompts) help you brainstorm starting keywords for any niche, including variants, synonyms, and related concepts a tool alone might miss. Search intent analysis (10 prompts) teaches the AI to classify keywords as informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational — and to tell you which content format wins for each. Long-tail discovery (8 prompts) digs for specific, low-competition phrases with question modifiers, comparisons, and problem-solution framing. Competitor keyword gaps (8 prompts) turn rival sites into idea factories by extracting the topics they rank for that you don't. SERP analysis (7 prompts) reads the results page for you — sniffing out weak results, snippet opportunities, and intent mismatches you can exploit. Topic clustering (7 prompts) groups your keyword list into content pillars and supporting articles so you build topical authority instead of random one-off posts.

Every prompt includes [bracketed] placeholders showing exactly what to customize: your niche, your seed keyword, your competitor's URL, your audience. Copy, fill, paste.

## How to use these prompts

1. Start with the seed keyword prompts to build a raw list of ideas for your niche — don't filter yet, just collect everything.
2. Paste your list into the intent classification prompts so every keyword gets matched to the right content format before you write a word.
3. Run the long-tail discovery prompts on your best seed terms to expand each one into five or ten winnable variations.
4. Verify the shortlisted keywords in a real keyword research tool for search volume and difficulty — AI invents ideas, tools verify them.
5. Use the topic clustering prompts to organize your verified keywords into pillar pages and supporting articles, then build your content calendar from the clusters.

AI accelerates research; it doesn't replace judgment. Always cross-check volumes and look at the actual SERP before investing in content.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do these prompts work with free AI tools?**

Yes. They are plain-language prompts with bracketed customization slots, so they work identically in free and paid AI chatbots. No special features or plugins are required.

**Will AI-generated keywords be accurate?**

The ideas are a starting point, not verified data. AI can suggest phrasing that looks like a real search query but gets zero traffic. Always paste promising suggestions into a keyword tool to confirm people actually search for them.

**How many of these prompts should I use per project?**

Use the full sequence for a new site or niche: seeds first, then intent, then long-tails, then clustering. For routine content planning on an established site, the intent and clustering prompts alone handle most of the work.
