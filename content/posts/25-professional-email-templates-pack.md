## How to use these templates

1. Pick the template matching your situation — don't force a follow-up template into an apology.
2. Fill every bracket with specifics; a template with placeholders left in is worse than no template.
3. Read it aloud once: if it sounds robotic, adjust one sentence into your natural voice.
4. Check the three essentials before sending: clear subject line, one specific ask, and a deadline or next step.
5. For sensitive emails, sleep on the draft or have someone else read it — templates help with structure, not judgment.

The templates handle structure; you supply the specifics and the sincerity. An email that is specific, brief, and human gets answered — the template just gets you there faster.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I follow up without sounding pushy?**
Add new value or context each time ("thought you might find this relevant..."), keep it to one gentle nudge per week, and after three unanswered follow-ups, close the loop politely rather than chasing forever.

**What should never go in a professional email?**
Anything you wouldn't want forwarded: venting about colleagues, confidential numbers, sarcasm (it never lands in text), and all-caps. When in doubt about tone, pick up the phone instead.
