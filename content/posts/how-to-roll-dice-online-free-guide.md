## Dice notation cheat sheet

Tabletop gamers write rolls in a compact notation worth learning:

- **1d6** — roll one six-sided die
- **3d6** — roll three six-sided dice, add them up
- **1d20+5** — roll a twenty-sided die, add 5 to the result
- **2d8-1** — roll two eight-sided dice, subtract 1
- **d100 or d%** — percentile roll, 1 to 100 (often two D10s, one for tens)
- **4d6 drop lowest** — roll four six-sided dice, discard the lowest (the classic D&D ability-score method)

Our online roller understands the count-plus-modifier pattern, so `3d6+2` style rolls are one tap instead of mental math.
