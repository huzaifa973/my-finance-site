Post the wrong size and Instagram will do it for you — by zooming in, cropping your edges, and softening your image. Stories are still one of the highest-engagement surfaces on the platform, and the difference between a crisp, professional-looking story and a blurry cropped one usually comes down to a single number: **1080 × 1920 pixels**. This guide gives you the exact dimensions, the safe zones most guides skip, the file specs that keep quality high, and a free way to resize any photo to fit perfectly.

## The exact Instagram story dimensions

Instagram stories use a **9:16 vertical aspect ratio** — the tall rectangle of a phone held upright. The optimal export size is:

- **1080 pixels wide × 1920 pixels tall**
- Aspect ratio: **9:16**
- File formats: JPG or PNG (JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with text)
- Keep files under 30 MB (stories handle much larger files than feed posts, but smaller uploads process faster)

Exporting at exactly 1080 × 1920 matters because Instagram does not upscale gracefully. If you upload a smaller image, it stretches it to fill the frame and the result looks soft. If you upload something larger, Instagram compresses it — usually fine, but you lose control of the final quality. Export at the native size and you get the sharpest possible result.

## The safe zones: what Instagram covers up

This is where most story graphics go wrong. Instagram overlays its own interface on top of your story:

- **Top ~250 px:** your profile picture, name, and the story progress bars
- **Bottom ~250 px:** the reply field, like button, and send/share icons
- **Side edges:** a small margin gets cropped on some devices

Keep faces, headlines, logos, and call-to-action text inside the **central ~1080 × 1420 px zone**. Treat the top and bottom bands as decoration-only areas — gradients and background colors are fine there, but nothing important. This single habit separates amateur stories from professional ones.

## Portrait photos, landscape photos, and square posts

What you do depends on what you are starting with:

**Portrait phone photos (4:5, 3:4, or 9:16):** You are close. Resize to fill 1080 × 1920 and crop a small amount off the top or bottom. Minimal loss.

**Landscape photos (16:9 or 3:2):** These are the problem case. To fill a 9:16 frame you either crop away most of the photo or accept letterboxing (blurred or solid-color bars top and bottom). The stylish option: place the landscape photo in the middle of the 1080 × 1920 canvas and add a blurred, enlarged copy of the same image as the background fill. It looks intentional and keeps the whole photo visible.

**Square posts (1:1):** Same treatment as landscape — center on the canvas with a designed background.

**Text graphics (Canva, screenshots):** Design at 1080 × 1920 from the start so text stays inside the safe zone. PNG format keeps text edges crisp.

## Quality tips for crisp stories

- **Start with a high-resolution source.** A photo taken at full resolution survives resizing; a screenshot of a screenshot will not.
- **Export JPG at 85–90% quality** for photos — the sweet spot between sharpness and file size.
- **Use PNG for graphics with text or flat colors** to avoid compression artifacts around lettering.
- **Do not upload, download, and re-upload.** Every Instagram compression pass degrades quality. Resize once, upload once.
- **Check the safe zone on a real phone** after your first few stories — interface layouts shift slightly across app versions.

## Story dimensions for other placements

Stories are not the only vertical surface worth knowing. If you repurpose story content, keep these sibling sizes handy:

- **Reels:** also 1080 × 1920 (9:16) — a story graphic drops straight into a Reel cover or clip
- **Feed portrait posts:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5) — crop your story design's center band and it fits the feed
- **Profile highlights covers:** 1080 × 1920, but only the center circle (~845 px diameter) shows — keep icons centered

Designing once at 1080 × 1920 and deriving the other sizes from it saves hours if you post across formats daily.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the exact Instagram story size in pixels?

1080 × 1920 pixels — a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio. This is the full-HD resolution Instagram stories display at, and resizing your images to exactly this size gives the sharpest result.

### Why does Instagram crop or zoom my story photos?

Instagram forces everything into 9:16. If your photo is a different shape, it zooms and crops to fill the frame, cutting off edges. Resizing or letterboxing your image to 1080 × 1920 beforehand keeps everything visible.

### What is the Instagram story safe zone?

Keep key text and faces inside the central area: leave about 250 px of clear space at the top (below the profile bar) and 250 px at the bottom (above the reply box). Instagram's UI overlays cover those bands.
