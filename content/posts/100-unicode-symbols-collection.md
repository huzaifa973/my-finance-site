## Frequently asked questions

**Will unicode symbols work in Instagram bios and TikTok captions?**
Yes. These are plain text characters, so they work anywhere text works — bios, captions, comments, and headings. Stick to widely supported symbols (arrows, stars, hearts) since exotic ones may render as boxes on older devices.

**Do unicode symbols hurt SEO?**
Used sparingly, no. One symbol in a title or heading is fine. But stuffing symbols everywhere looks spammy to both readers and search engines, so keep it to one accent per line.

**Why do some symbols look different on my phone?**
Some characters have both text and emoji presentations (like ❤). Phones often render the emoji version while desktops show plain text. Test important uses on both before publishing.
