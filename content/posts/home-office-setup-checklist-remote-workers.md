Working from home fails quietly: a bad chair hurts by week three, bad lighting ruins every video call, and no boundary between work and home means neither ever ends. This twenty-five-point checklist covers the setup that actually matters — desk and chair, lighting and sound, internet and power, and the boundaries that protect your evenings. It suits new remote workers, and anyone whose “temporary” home setup quietly became permanent.

## What's inside this resource

The checks are ordered by impact: body first (chair, desk, screen), then calls (light, sound, background), then infrastructure (internet, power, backup), then boundaries (hours, space, shutdown ritual). Fix them in order and the biggest pains go first.

## The resource (sample)

1. Chair supports your lower back and lets feet rest flat on the floor.
2. Screen top is at eye level; you are not looking up or down all day.
3. Keyboard and mouse keep wrists straight and shoulders relaxed.
4. Light comes from the front or side for video calls, never from behind you.
5. A headset with a microphone beats laptop audio for every call.
6. Internet is wired or reliably fast, with a backup plan for outages.
7. Power strips protect your equipment and keep cables tidy.
8. Your workspace has a door — or at least a clear boundary.
9. Work hours are set, and a shutdown ritual ends the day.
10. You can stand and move at least once an hour.

## How to use this resource

1. Work through the list in order, from body to boundaries.
2. Fix pain first: if something hurts, it outranks everything else on the list.
3. Test video and audio with a real call before an important meeting.
4. Revisit the list after a month — setups drift, and so do habits.
5. Spend on the chair before the monitor: comfort compounds daily.

A resource is only useful when it gets used — pick one item today, use it as written, and adapt it from experience rather than from theory.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an expensive chair?**

Not expensive — supportive. A chair that keeps your back supported and feet flat matters more than brand or price; try before you buy.

**What is the best lighting for video calls?**

Soft light on your face from the front, from a window or a lamp. Light behind you turns you into a silhouette.

**How do I separate work from home?**

A shutdown ritual: close the laptop, clear the desk, and change out of work mode. The ritual matters more than the room.
