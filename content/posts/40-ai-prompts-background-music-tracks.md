## How to use this pack

1. Pick the category that fits your project — lofi for study content, cinematic for trailers, ambient for meditation, corporate for explainers, tension for drama.
2. Copy a prompt from the download file and paste it into your AI music tool of choice.
3. Set the duration the tool allows (30 seconds for loops, longer for full beds).
4. Generate two or three variations and keep the one with the cleanest loop point.
5. If the result is too busy, append "minimal arrangement, no vocals, subtle mix" to the prompt and regenerate.
6. Export in WAV for video editing, and always confirm the license tier of your tool before publishing.

Most generators respond well to small edits, so treat each prompt as a starting point: change one instrument or the tempo and you often get a track that fits your project much better than the first output.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do these prompts include vocals or lyrics?**
No — every prompt is written for instrumental background music, with "no vocals" implied in the arrangement notes. Instrumental beds are what you want under dialogue, narration, or gameplay, and they loop far more cleanly than vocal tracks.

**How long should the generated track be?**
For background beds, aim for at least 60 to 90 seconds so the loop is not obvious; many generators extend from a 30-second seed. For intros, stingers, and transitions, 10 to 30 seconds is plenty — generate short and fade the tail in your editor.
