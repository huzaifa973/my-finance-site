## How to use this pack

1. Download the full pack using the button below and pick the style that matches your video.
2. Copy the prompt and paste it into any AI image generator, setting the aspect ratio to 16:9.
3. Generate two or three variations and pick the one with the clearest focal point.
4. Add your title text in a free editor like Canva or Photopea — keep it to three or four bold words.
5. Test the thumbnail at small size: if it is not readable as a tiny preview, simplify it.

The biggest thumbnail mistake is clutter. At feed size, viewers see your thumbnail at roughly the size of a postage stamp, so one strong face, one bold object, and a few big words will always beat a busy scene. Run your finished thumbnail through a quick zoom-out test before uploading — squint at it from arm's length. If the emotion and the main object still read clearly, it is ready.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do these thumbnail prompts work with free AI image generators?**
Yes. They are written in plain descriptive language that works with any text-to-image tool. For best results, generate at a 16:9 aspect ratio so the image fits YouTube's thumbnail dimensions without awkward cropping.

**Can I add text to the generated thumbnails?**
AI image tools often garble small text, so it is usually better to generate the background, face, or product shot, then add your title text in a free editor like Canva or Photopea. That gives you crisp, readable letters in your channel's own font.

**What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?**
YouTube displays thumbnails at 1280 x 720 pixels, a 16:9 ratio. Generate or crop your image to that ratio so nothing important gets cut off, and keep key elements away from the very edges.
