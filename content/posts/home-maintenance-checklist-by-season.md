Small home problems become expensive home disasters on a predictable schedule — the gutter that overflows one spring rots the fascia by fall, and the furnace filter nobody changed in October fails in January. The fix is not heroics; it is rhythm. This free home maintenance checklist by season gives you four quarterly lists — spring, summer, fall, and winter — covering roofs, HVAC, plumbing, safety devices, and the exterior. Each task is written as a single checkable action, so you can work a whole season's list in a weekend. Homeowners, new buyers, and landlords get the same system professionals use: do the small things on time, and the big things rarely happen.

## What's inside this checklist

The full checklist holds 44 tasks across the four seasons, ordered the way the year actually flows. Spring (12 tasks) is recovery and preparation: roof inspection after winter, gutter cleaning, AC servicing before the heat, and waking up outdoor plumbing safely. Summer (10 tasks) is the light-maintenance season: monthly filter changes, deck and fence checks, and moisture watch in basements. Fall (12 tasks) is winterization: furnace servicing, chimney cleaning, weatherstripping, and shutting down outdoor water. Winter (10 tasks) is protection mode: freeze prevention, ice-dam watch, and mid-season safety checks. Nothing requires a contractor unless you want one — most tasks need basic tools and an afternoon.

## How to use this checklist

1. Download the full checklist and print the current season's page, or copy it into a notes app.
2. Schedule one weekend per season — put all four dates in your calendar now so they actually happen.
3. Work top to bottom; the lists are ordered from most damage-preventing to nice-to-have.
4. Note anything you cannot do yourself and book it early — HVAC techs and chimney sweeps fill up fast in fall.
5. When a season is done, file the checked list; next year you will know exactly what you did and when.

The calendar step is the one that matters. A checklist in a drawer helps no one; four calendar appointments turn maintenance into something that simply happens.

## Frequently asked questions

**How often should I do home maintenance checks?**
Seasonally — four focused sessions a year. Each season's list takes a weekend at most, and catching small issues early is what prevents the five-figure repairs that blindside homeowners.

**What is the most commonly skipped home maintenance task?**
Dryer vent cleaning and water heater flushing. Both are out of sight, both cause real damage when neglected — lint buildup is a fire risk, sediment kills heaters — and both take under an hour.

**Do renters need this checklist too?**
Parts of it, yes. Safety devices, dryer vents, and filter changes often fall to whoever lives there — check your lease. Flag structural or system issues to your landlord early, in writing, with dates.
