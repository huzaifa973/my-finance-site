## How to use this pack

1. Pick the template group that matches your article format: how-to for tutorials, listicle for roundups, comparison for versus posts, question for explainers, numbers-driven for data posts.
2. Fill each [bracket] with your specific keyword, product, number, or audience — keep fillers short so the final title stays under 60 characters.
3. Put your primary keyword as close to the front of the title as the template allows.
4. Read the finished title aloud: if it sounds like a robot wrote it, swap in a more natural filler word.
5. Test two template variants as title tags over time in Search Console and keep the one with the higher click-through rate.

Templates are starting points, not finished headlines. The bloggers who get the most from this pack customize the fillers for their niche's vocabulary — the pattern does the structural work, your specifics do the persuading.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will using templates make my titles look generic?**
Only if you fill the brackets generically. The template supplies the proven structure; your specific numbers, products, and audience details supply the uniqueness. Two bloggers using the same template with different fillers produce completely different headlines.

**Can I use these titles for YouTube videos too?**
Absolutely. Search-driven headline psychology works the same on YouTube, Pinterest, and anywhere else people search. Just keep video titles slightly shorter and front-load the most clickable words.
