A clean invoice gets you paid faster. Clients process clear, professional invoices quickly and sit on confusing ones — missing numbers, vague descriptions, or no payment details are the most common reasons freelance payments stall. You do not need invoicing software to fix this; you need the right words in the right places.

This free template pack gives you 7 copy-paste invoice templates built for freelancers: services, hourly work, milestone projects, retainers, proforma invoices, and the two follow-up messages every freelancer eventually needs. Each template is plain text, formatted for a clean PDF export, with bracketed placeholders you fill in once. Pick the template, fill the brackets, export to PDF, send.

## How to use this template pack

1. Pick the template that matches how you bill: fixed-price, hourly, milestone, retainer, or proforma for quotes.
2. Fill in every [BRACKET] placeholder, and give the invoice a sequential number you have never used before.
3. Export or print the finished document as a PDF, and name the file clearly, e.g. `INV-2026-001-clientname.pdf`.
4. Send it with a short, friendly email — state the amount, the due date, and the payment link in the message body too.
5. Calendar the due date: send template 6 the day after it passes if unpaid, and template 7 the moment payment lands.

Two habits separate freelancers who get paid on time from those who chase money. First, invoice the day the work is done, not at the end of the month — delay signals that the money is not urgent. Second, keep your payment terms on every invoice and enforce them politely; clients respect the boundaries you actually hold.

## Frequently asked questions

**What must a freelance invoice include to be legally valid?**
The essentials are your name and contact details, the client's name, a unique invoice number, the invoice date, a clear description of the work, the amount due, and your payment details. Add your tax ID and the correct tax lines for your country — VAT, GST, or sales tax rules differ, so check your local requirements once and reuse the same format on every invoice.

**How do I number my invoices?**
Use one simple sequential system such as INV-2026-001, INV-2026-002, and never skip or reuse a number. Sequential numbering keeps your bookkeeping clean, makes tax time painless, and looks professional. If you also send quotes, use a separate QUO prefix so the two sequences never collide.

**When should I send a late payment reminder?**
Send a polite reminder the day after the due date — most late payments are forgetfulness, not refusal. Follow up with a firmer message at 14 days overdue, and a final notice at 30 days that references your late fee policy. Having template 6 ready in advance turns an awkward conversation into a two-minute task.
