Starting a blog is easy. Filling it week after week is the hard part — especially when you are new and every idea feels either taken or too big. This list of one hundred blog post ideas is built for beginners: every idea is something a first-time blogger can actually write, with no expert credentials, no budget, and no audience required. They cover the full beginner journey, from setting up and choosing a niche to getting your first readers and building a habit that lasts. Pick any idea, make it your own, and publish — done beats perfect, every time. You do not need a perfect niche, a perfect design, or a perfect first post — you need a published post, and then another one. Use this list to remove the blank-page problem entirely, so your energy goes into writing instead of wondering what to write about.

## What's inside this resource

The ideas are grouped by theme so you can plan in batches. The first groups cover setup, SEO basics, and writing skills — the foundations every new blogger needs. The middle groups give you traffic-friendly formats like list posts, tutorials, and comparisons. Later groups are niche starters for travel, food, finance, parenting, fitness, and more, so you can find ideas that fit your topic. The final group is about momentum: habits, burnout, and keeping going when growth feels slow. Mix formats across weeks so your blog never feels like the same post on repeat.

## How to use this resource

1. Pick five ideas that match your niche and your experience, then schedule one per week for the next month.
2. Rewrite each idea in your own words and angle it toward your readers' questions, not generic advice.
3. Start with the easiest idea on your shortlist — an early published post builds confidence faster than a perfect draft.
4. After publishing, note which formats got comments or shares, and lean into those formats next month.
5. Revisit this list every quarter: ideas you skipped may fit better once you have a few months of experience.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many of these ideas should I publish per week?

One or two quality posts a week is plenty for a beginner. Consistency matters more than volume — a blog updated weekly for a year beats one that burns out in a month.

### Can I use these ideas if my niche is unusual?

Yes. Most ideas are format templates — swap in your niche and your experience, and the idea becomes specific to your blog.

### Should I write the setup posts first?

If your blog is brand new, yes. Setup and beginner-guide posts attract other beginners searching for exactly that help, and they build your foundation content.
