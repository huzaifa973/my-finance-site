Moving is consistently ranked as one of life's most stressful events, and most of that stress comes from forgotten tasks, not heavy boxes. A last-minute scramble for packing tape, a missed address change, a mover who shows up while you are still half-packed — all avoidable with a timeline. This free 120-item moving checklist walks you through every stage: 8 weeks out, 6 weeks, 4 weeks, 2 weeks, 1 week, moving day itself, and the first week after the move. Print it, check things off, and move like someone who has done it a dozen times.

## What's inside this pack

This is not a vague list of "remember to pack" reminders. The 120 items are sequenced the way a move actually unfolds: early weeks handle decluttering, quotes, and paperwork while there is still time to fix problems; middle weeks cover packing systems, notifications, and utility transfers; the final week is pure execution — confirmations, essentials boxes, and cleaning. The first-week-after section catches the things people forget once the truck is gone, like updating documents and checking for damage. Whether you are moving across town or across the country, renting or owning, solo or with a family, the timeline keeps you ahead of the chaos instead of behind it.

## How to use this pack

1. Download the full 120-item checklist and print it, or keep it on your phone.
2. Find your move date and count back eight weeks — that is your start line.
3. Work one timeline block at a time, checking items off as you finish them.
4. Star any item with a deadline (notice periods, bookings) so it never slips.
5. Share the list with everyone helping you move so tasks do not get duplicated.
6. After the move, keep the checked list with your records — it is proof of what you did and when.

The real power of a timeline checklist is psychological: instead of one giant scary move, you get a handful of small weekly jobs that are each easy to finish.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many boxes will I need?**
A rough rule: 10 to 15 medium boxes per room for an average home, plus wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes and small heavy-duty boxes for books. Buy 10 percent more than you estimate — running out mid-pack is the classic moving-day disaster.

**What should go in the essentials box?**
Everything you need for the first 24 hours without opening other boxes: phone chargers, toiletries, medications, toilet paper, basic tools, snacks, water, a change of clothes, bedding, and important documents. Keep it in your own vehicle, not the moving truck.
