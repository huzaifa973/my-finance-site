Getting Google AdSense approval in Pakistan feels like a moving target. One year a ten-page blog sails through, the next a forty-page site gets rejected with a generic "site does not comply with policies" email. The difference is rarely luck. Google's reviewers apply the same global policy checklist to every application, but Pakistani sites trip over a predictable set of issues: missing legal pages, thin or copied content, traffic from click-exchange groups, and payout setup confusion. This guide breaks the process into the exact requirements reviewers check, the seventeen most effective fixes, and what to do after approval, including Ezoic and Mediavine thresholds and Payoneer payout setup for Pakistan.
## What Google actually checks before approving your site
Reviewers evaluate the whole property, not a single landing page, so every corner of your domain must look like a real, finished publication. Start with the four mandatory pages. A privacy policy page is non-negotiable and must describe how you collect and use data, including cookies and advertising. An about page should name a real person or team, explain who runs the site, and why readers should trust you. A contact page with a working email or contact form proves you are reachable. A terms of service or disclaimer page is expected on any site that will display ads. Beyond pages, your content must be original: copied paragraphs, spun articles, and product descriptions lifted from manufacturers are the fastest route to rejection. Aim for at least fifteen substantial articles, each over 800 words, before you apply. Navigation must be clean, every link must work, and there should be no "coming soon" or "under construction" pages anywhere on the domain.
## Traffic sources that help — and the ones that get you rejected
Google does not require a minimum visitor count, but it does look at where your traffic comes from. Organic search traffic from Google and Bing is the strongest signal because it proves your content answers real queries. Social traffic from Facebook, Pinterest, and WhatsApp groups is fine as long as it is genuine. What gets Pakistani sites flagged is artificial traffic: click-exchange Telegram and WhatsApp groups where members click each other's ads, paid-to-click services, auto-refresh scripts, and repeatedly clicking your own ads from the same IP. These trigger invalid activity detections and can get your application denied or, worse, your account banned after approval. If you have used any of these methods, stop at least thirty days before applying and let your traffic settle to natural sources.

## Why Pakistani sites get rejected — and the fix for each reason
The three most common rejection emails in Pakistan map to three fixable problems. "Invalid click activity" means Google detected artificial clicks or traffic. The fix is to stop all exchange traffic, place ads only after approval, and wait a full month for your traffic pattern to normalize before reapplying. "Site under construction" means reviewers found empty pages, placeholder text, broken navigation, or a domain that recently changed hands. The fix is to finish every page, remove anything labeled coming soon, and let the site sit with fresh content for two weeks before reapplying. "Copyrighted content" means your articles, images, or videos were copied from another site. The fix is to rewrite every paragraph in your own words, replace stock images with ones you have licensed or created yourself, and remove any embedded content you do not have rights to. If you get a generic policy rejection without a specific reason, the issue is almost always thin content: expand your shortest posts to over 800 words and add five more articles before trying again.
## Premium ad network alternatives: AdX, Ezoic, and Mediavine
Once AdSense is approved and your traffic grows, you can earn significantly more by moving to a premium ad network that has access to Google AdX demand. Ezoic is the easiest first step: it accepts sites with as little as 10,000 monthly page views and typically doubles or triples AdSense revenue through automated ad testing. Mediavine requires 50,000 monthly sessions (not page views) and offers higher CPMs but a stricter application. AdThrive and Raptive require even more traffic, usually over 100,000 monthly page views, and pay the highest rates. For Pakistani publishers, the realistic ladder is AdSense first, then Ezoic once you cross 10,000 page views, then Mediavine once you hit 50,000 sessions. All three pay via Payoneer or direct bank transfer, so your payout setup works across every network.
## Setting up payouts in Pakistan: Payoneer and JazzCash
AdSense pays out when your balance reaches $100, and the only reliable method for most Pakistani publishers is Payoneer. Sign up for a Payoneer account with your real CNIC details, receive a virtual US receiving account, and link that account's routing and account numbers to your AdSense payment profile. When AdSense sends the wire, it lands in your Payoneer USD balance, and you can withdraw it to any Pakistani bank account at the interbank rate or load it onto a Payoneer debit card for ATM withdrawals. JazzCash does not directly receive AdSense wires, but some publishers withdraw Payoneer funds to a bank account linked to JazzCash for easier mobile spending. Remember to verify your address with the Google PIN letter, which arrives by regular mail in Pakistan within two to four weeks, and to submit your tax information in the AdSense payment center to avoid withholding.
## Frequently asked questions
**How many posts do I need before applying for AdSense?**
There is no official number, but fifteen substantial original articles is the safe minimum for Pakistani sites. Each should be over 800 words, cover a distinct topic, and be free of copied text. Reviewers want to see a complete publication, not a skeleton with three posts.
**Can I use a free Blogspot domain for AdSense approval?**
It is possible but slower and less reliable. A custom domain costs only a few dollars a year and signals to reviewers that you are a serious publisher. Most approved Pakistani sites in 2026 use a custom domain on WordPress or a static host.
**Will a VPN or changing my IP affect approval?**
Google knows you are applying from Pakistan based on your billing address and phone number. Using a VPN to appear in another country does not help and can raise fraud flags. Apply honestly from Pakistan with matching address and ID details.
