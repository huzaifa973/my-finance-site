## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between A4 and US Letter?**

A4 is 210 by 297 mm and US Letter is 215.9 by 279.4 mm, so Letter is slightly wider but shorter. If you print an A4 document on Letter paper without scaling, the bottom gets cut off, which is exactly why having both on one chart matters.

**What DPI should I use for a printable PDF?**

Use 300 DPI for anything going to a print shop or meant to look professional. 150 DPI is acceptable for internal office documents and home printers. 72 DPI is fine for PDFs that will only ever be viewed on a screen.

**Why do my exported PDFs look blurry when printed?**

Almost always because the export used screen resolution (72 or 96 DPI) instead of 300 DPI. Re-export using the pixel dimensions from the chart's 300 DPI column and the blur disappears.
