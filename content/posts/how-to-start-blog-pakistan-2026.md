Starting a blog in Pakistan has never been cheaper or more accessible. You can register a domain for a few thousand rupees, host the entire site for free on a global content delivery network, and reach readers in over a hundred countries without ever meeting an advertiser. The catch is that the easy part is over in an afternoon; the hard part — publishing useful content week after week — is where most people quit. This guide walks you through every technical and strategic decision so the setup does not waste your time, and you can spend your energy on the writing that actually earns money.

## Why start a blog in Pakistan in 2026

Three trends make this a strong moment for Pakistani bloggers. First, English-language content from South Asian writers is increasingly ranking for global queries because Google rewards depth and local expertise over generic articles. Second, payment infrastructure has matured: Payoneer, Wise, JazzCash, and Easypaisa together cover almost every way an advertiser or affiliate network might pay you. Third, static site generators and free edge hosting have eliminated the monthly hosting bill that used to eat into early earnings. A blogger in Lahore or Karachi now has the same publishing infrastructure as a blogger in London, at a fraction of the living cost.

## Step 1: Pick a niche with earning potential

Your niche decides whether you earn dollars or pennies. Choose a topic where readers have purchase intent, where advertisers bid high, and where you can write 50 articles without running out of ideas. Strong niches for Pakistani bloggers include personal finance in Pakistan, tech product reviews, travel within Pakistan, career and exam guidance, cooking, Islamic finance, and SaaS tool comparisons. Avoid ultra-broad niches like "technology" or "health" — you will compete with established media sites. Instead narrow to something like "budget laptops for students in Pakistan" or "Halal investing for Pakistani expats." Validate your niche by checking that at least ten related keywords get search volume and that affiliate programs exist for products in that space.

## Step 2: Choose and register your domain

A domain is your only mandatory expense. A .com domain typically costs 2,500 to 4,000 PKR per year from local registrars like HosterPK, Naqash, or PakHost, or around $10 from Namecheap or Cloudflare. The .pk ccTLD costs roughly 1,500 to 3,000 PKR per year and signals local relevance, but it limits your global perception. For most bloggers targeting international traffic and AdSense, a .com is the better long-term choice. Pick a .pk only if your audience is exclusively Pakistani and you want local SEO benefits. Avoid hyphens, numbers, and trademarked words. Buy the domain directly from the registrar rather than through a hosting reseller so you keep control if you switch hosts.

## Step 3: Hosting options for Pakistani bloggers

You have two solid paths. The first is static hosting with Hugo on Cloudflare Pages, which is completely free, includes unlimited bandwidth, automatic HTTPS, and global edge delivery. It is the fastest, most secure option, and it costs nothing to run. The second is shared WordPress hosting from local providers, starting around 300 to 800 PKR per month, which gives you a visual editor and thousands of plugins but requires maintenance and can be slow if the server is overcrowded. For a new blogger on a budget, start with Cloudflare Pages and Hugo — you can always migrate to WordPress later if you need its features.

## Step 4: Install WordPress or set up Hugo

If you choose WordPress, buy shared hosting, use the one-click installer in cPanel, pick a lightweight theme like GeneratePress or Astra, and install only essential plugins: an SEO plugin, a caching plugin, and a backup plugin. If you choose Hugo, install the Hugo binary, create a new site, pick a theme from the Hugo showcase, write your posts in Markdown, push the repository to GitHub or GitLab, and connect that repository to Cloudflare Pages. Every git push rebuilds and redeploys your site in under a minute. Both approaches take an afternoon to set up; Hugo rewards you with zero maintenance and zero hosting bills forever.

## Step 5: Essential pages every blog needs

Before you publish posts, create four pages. An About page that tells your story and establishes expertise — readers and advertisers both check this. A Contact page with a working email or contact form so brands can reach you for sponsorships. A Privacy Policy and Terms page, required by AdSense and most affiliate networks, and you can generate one free online. A Start Here or Resources page that funnels new visitors to your best articles. These pages take an hour to write and make your blog look professional instead of like a abandoned project.

## Step 6: SEO basics to get traffic from Google

SEO is how readers find you. Install an SEO plugin or configure your Hugo theme to output proper title tags and meta descriptions. Do keyword research for every post — target long-tail questions with low competition rather than single high-volume words. Use your primary keyword in the title, first paragraph, one subheading, and the URL. Write genuinely useful, long-form content; 1,200-word articles that answer the question completely outrank thin 300-word posts. Link internally between your articles to spread authority. Submit your sitemap to Google Search Console and check it weekly. Pakistani bloggers often overlook page speed, but Google Core Web Vitals matter more every year — static sites on Cloudflare Pages score near-perfectly with no effort.

## Step 7: Monetization: AdSense, affiliate, and sponsored posts

Once you have 20 to 30 published posts and steady traffic, apply for Google AdSense. Approval typically takes a few weeks and requires original content, the essential pages above, and no policy violations. AdSense earnings for a Pakistani blog vary widely, but a site with 50,000 monthly pageviews can earn $300 to $1,000 per month depending on niche. Affiliate marketing often pays better per visitor: join Amazon Associates, Daraz Affiliate Program, or niche SaaS affiliate programs, and write honest product reviews with your links. Sponsored posts from Pakistani brands pay 10,000 to 50,000 PKR per post once you have an engaged audience, and they pay directly to your bank or mobile wallet.

## Getting paid in Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa, Payoneer, Wise

Receiving international payments is the part that confuses new Pakistani bloggers most. Google AdSense supports direct bank transfer to Pakistani rupee accounts and Western Union payouts. For affiliate networks and freelance platforms, Payoneer is the standard: it gives you a US receiving account, a virtual debit card, and the ability to withdraw to any Pakistani bank. Wise (formerly TransferWise) is excellent for GBP and EUR payouts with low conversion fees. PayPal is not officially available in Pakistan, but many networks pay to Payoneer instead. For local Pakistani sponsors and small ad deals, JazzCash and Easypaisa mobile wallets are instant, free to receive, and familiar to every local brand. Keep a spreadsheet of every payout for tax records.

## Your first 90 days

Publish one post per week for the first twelve weeks. Do not check earnings daily — there will be none. Instead, track indexed pages, keyword rankings, and total words published. By day 90 you will have a dozen articles, a professional site, and the habit that separates bloggers who earn from those who quit. The setup in this guide takes one weekend. The next five years of writing is up to you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know coding to start a blog in Pakistan?**
No. WordPress requires no coding at all, and even Hugo beginners can follow a written tutorial without writing a line of JavaScript. The free hosting route is technical but well-documented, and every step is a one-time setup.

**Is WordPress or Hugo better for a Pakistani beginner?**
WordPress is easier to learn and has more tutorials in Urdu and English. Hugo is faster, free to host forever, and more secure. If you are comfortable with files and git, choose Hugo. If you want a visual editor and plugins, choose WordPress on shared hosting.

**Can I run ads before getting AdSense approval?**
Yes, but the alternatives pay less. Media.net, Ezoic (requires 10,000 monthly sessions), and direct ad sales to local brands all work without AdSense. Most bloggers still target AdSense because it fills unsold inventory automatically and pays reliably to Pakistani accounts.

**How much traffic do I need before applying for AdSense?**
There is no official minimum traffic requirement, but sites with under 20 posts or very low traffic are often rejected for insufficient content. Aim for 25 to 30 original posts and a few hundred daily visitors before applying to maximize your chance of approval on the first try.
