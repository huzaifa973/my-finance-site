Your X header is the billboard above your profile — the first thing visitors see, and prime branding real estate. But X is unforgiving about dimensions: upload the wrong size and your carefully designed banner arrives blurry, cropped, or with your logo hiding behind your profile picture. Here are the exact 2026 dimensions, the safe-area rules nobody tells you about, and the fastest free way to get any image to fit perfectly.

## The exact X header dimensions

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Recommended size | **1500 × 500 px** |
| Aspect ratio | **3:1** |
| Minimum usable | 1024 × 342 px (will look soft) |
| Max file size | 2 MB (larger uploads may fail) |
| Best formats | JPG (photos), PNG (text/logos) |

**Upload at exactly 1500 × 500.** X does not upscale gracefully — a 1024-wide image stretched to fill the header looks noticeably soft on desktop. Starting at the recommended size keeps text crisp and photos sharp on every screen.

For reference, the other X image specs you will touch: profile picture 400 × 400 px (displayed as a circle), in-stream images 1600 × 900 px (16:9).

## The safe area: what actually stays visible

Here is the part most size guides skip: **X does not show your whole header on all devices.** The visible area changes between desktop, mobile, and the profile overlay:

- **Keep critical content in the center.** Place logos, headlines, and faces in the middle ~60% of the banner width. Edges get cropped on narrower screens.
- **Watch the bottom-left.** Your circular profile picture overlaps the header's bottom-left corner — roughly a 200 × 200 px zone at 1500 × 500 scale. Never put text or faces there.
- **Top strip caution.** On some views the top ~50 px can sit close to browser chrome. Keep the top edge decorative, not informational.
- **Test on mobile.** Over half of X browsing is mobile, where the header displays shorter. Preview your profile on your phone after uploading — it takes ten seconds and catches most cropping surprises.

A simple mental model: design as if only the **center 900 × 400 px** is guaranteed, and treat everything outside it as bleed.

## Design tips for a header that works

- **One message.** A header is glanced at for two seconds — one tagline, one logo, one visual. Banners crammed with text get ignored.
- **Match your brand colors** to your profile picture and pinned post for a coherent profile.
- **Leave breathing room.** Text needs padding from edges; cramped text looks amateur even at perfect dimensions.
- **Dark or light?** Dark headers make white profile pictures pop and look premium; light headers feel open and friendly. Either works — pick one and commit.
- **Update seasonally.** Product launch, event, holiday — a fresh header signals an active account. Keep the master file so updates take minutes.
