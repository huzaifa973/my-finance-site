## How to use this pack

1. Choose the prompt group that matches your task: summarize for overviews, extract for data, Q&A for interrogation, contracts for review triage, invoices for bookkeeping, academic for research.
2. Get the text into the AI: upload the PDF directly, or copy-paste the text if the tool has upload limits.
3. Replace the bracketed instruction with your pasted text, keeping the prompt's format requirements intact — the output format is what makes these prompts powerful.
4. Verify critical outputs: spot-check dates, amounts, and clause references against the original PDF before acting on them.
5. For long documents, summarize in chunks first and then run a synthesis prompt over the chunk summaries.

The prompts do the thinking about structure so you can focus on judgment. They are fastest as a first pass — triage with AI, then spend your own attention only on the parts that matter.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it safe to upload sensitive PDFs to AI tools?**
Treat AI tools like any cloud service: avoid uploading documents with passwords, full bank details, or confidential client data unless you trust the provider and have checked its data-retention policy. For sensitive contracts, redact personal identifiers before uploading or use a locally run model.

**What if the AI hallucinates details from my PDF?**
Reduce it with three habits: paste text instead of relying on the tool's PDF parsing, demand page or section citations in the prompt, and add "if the answer is not in the document, say so" to Q&A prompts. The prompts in this pack already include these guardrails.
