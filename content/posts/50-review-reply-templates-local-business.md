Reviews are the first thing many customers read about a local business — and your replies are part of the review. A thoughtful reply to a happy customer turns them into a regular; a calm, specific reply to an unhappy one shows every future reader how you handle problems. These fifty templates cover both sides: warm thank-yous, replies to detailed praise, and measured responses to complaints, so you never stare at a blank reply box again. They are written for owners and managers of restaurants, salons, clinics, shops, and service businesses.

## What's inside this resource

The templates are grouped by situation: short thanks for quick five-star reviews, warmer replies for detailed praise, and complaint replies that apologize once, name the issue, and move the conversation to email or phone. Each complaint template includes a contact placeholder — never argue details in public. The golden rule across all fifty: reply within a few days, use the reviewer's name when given, and keep it human, not corporate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I reply to every review?

Yes, including positive ones. Replies show you are active and listening, and they give future readers confidence. A short, genuine reply is enough for most five-star reviews.

### What should I never do in a reply?

Never argue, never share private customer details, and never offer a bribe for a changed review. Apologize once, offer a private channel, and move on.

### How fast should I reply?

Within two to three days is ideal. Fast replies look attentive; same-day replies to complaints can stop a small issue from becoming a bigger story.
