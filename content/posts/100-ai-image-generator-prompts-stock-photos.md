## How to use this pack

1. Download the full pack using the button below and pick the category you need.
2. Copy the prompt and paste it into any AI image generator.
3. Add a style tag like photorealistic or illustration if your tool supports it.
4. Generate, review the result, and regenerate with small word tweaks if needed.
5. For a consistent series, reuse the same lighting and style words across prompts.
6. Check your generator's license terms before using images commercially.

Most generators let you set an aspect ratio, so match it to your use case: wide for website headers, square for social posts, tall for stories and pins. If a result looks off, change one descriptive word at a time — lighting and camera-angle words have the biggest impact. Over time you will learn which phrases give you the exact look you want, and this prompt bank is the fastest way to build that vocabulary.
