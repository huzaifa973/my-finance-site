Local search runs on consistency. When your business name, address, and phone number match exactly everywhere — Google, Yelp, directories, and niche listings — search engines trust your listing more, and customers find you instead of a competitor. These fifty citation templates give you the exact formats to use, so you never have to guess how to write your details on a new directory again. They are built for small business owners, local marketers, and anyone managing listings for a shop, clinic, restaurant, or service business.

## What's inside this resource

The pack covers the major platforms first — Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook — then moves into industry directories, review sites, and the everyday places your NAP appears, from receipts and business cards to sponsorship pages. Every template uses the same NAP order (name, address, phone, website) so your listings stay identical wherever they appear. Consistency is the whole game: even small differences like 'St.' versus 'Street' can weaken the signal.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a citation in local SEO?

A citation is any online mention of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) — with or without a link. Search engines use matching citations to verify your business is real and located where you say it is.

### Do I need all 50 citations?

No. Start with the major platforms and your industry directories, then add the rest over time. Ten accurate, consistent listings beat fifty sloppy ones.

### How often should I check my citations?

Audit them twice a year, and immediately after any move, phone change, or rebrand. One outdated listing can confuse customers and search engines alike.
