## How to use this pack

1. Pick the hook style that fits your video's goal: curiosity for tips, contrarian for opinions, tutorial for how-tos, storytelling for personal content, list for roundups.
2. Copy a script and replace the bracketed placeholders with your topic.
3. Read it as your opening line or burn it in as on-screen text in the first second.
4. Film the payoff the hook promises. A great hook with a weak payoff kills retention.
5. Rotate styles across your uploads so your feed never feels repetitive.
6. Track which styles get the most watch time and double down on those.

A hook is a promise, not a trick. The scripts in this pack open loops and make claims, but your video still has to deliver. Use them to earn the first three seconds, then spend the rest of the video proving the opener right.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do hooks really make a difference?**

Yes. Platforms measure how many viewers watch past the first few seconds, and that early retention strongly influences distribution. A clear, curiosity-driven opener consistently outperforms starting with greetings or context.

**Can I use the same hook twice?**

You can reuse the structure but change the words. Viewers notice repeated openers, but the five styles in this pack give you fifty distinct starting points, so repetition is easy to avoid.
