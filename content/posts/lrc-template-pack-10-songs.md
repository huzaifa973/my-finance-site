LRC files are how lyrics sync to music in players and karaoke apps — but writing one from scratch means looking up the tag format, the timestamp syntax, and the structure every time. This pack gives you ten complete LRC templates, one per song, each with the metadata tags and a timed verse-chorus structure ready to fill with real lyrics. It is written for musicians releasing music, lyric video creators, and karaoke organizers who need synced lyrics without the format guesswork.

## What's inside this resource

Each template includes the standard tags — title, artist, and album — followed by timestamped lines in [mm:ss.xx] format, with an intro, two verses, and a chorus. The timestamps in the pack are placeholders showing the structure; replace them with the real times from your track. Most players accept LRC files with the same filename as the audio file, placed in the same folder — that one detail is what makes the lyrics appear.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an LRC file?

A plain-text file that pairs lyric lines with timestamps, so players can highlight each line as it is sung. It is the standard format for synced lyrics.

### How do I find the right timestamps?

Play the song and note the time each line starts, to the tenth of a second. Many lyric editors let you tap along in real time instead of typing numbers.

### Where do I put the LRC file?

Usually in the same folder as the audio file, with the same filename except the extension. Most players then load the lyrics automatically.
