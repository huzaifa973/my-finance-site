An out-of-office reply is a small message that does a big job: it tells people when you will respond, who can help instead, and that their email has not vanished into a void. Yet most people write theirs in a rush, the morning they leave, and forget the return date or the backup contact. These fifty templates remove that scramble. They cover vacation, sick leave, conferences, part-time schedules, and holiday closures, each in a professional tone that works for any workplace. Copy one, fill in the brackets, and you are done in under a minute. That minute is worth it: a good out-of-office reply protects your time off, because senders stop chasing you when they know exactly when you will return and who else can help. It also protects your professional reputation, since nobody feels ignored while you are away. Set it, test it, and then genuinely switch off — that is the whole point.

## What's inside this resource

The templates are grouped by situation so you can jump straight to the one you need. Vacation templates range from warm and friendly to strictly professional. Sick leave templates keep things brief and private — no details about your health, just dates and a contact. Conference templates explain delayed replies honestly. Part-time templates set clear expectations about working days. Holiday templates add a seasonal touch without losing the practical details. Every template includes the three essentials: when you return, who to contact instead, and a polite thank-you.

## How to use this resource

1. Pick the template that matches your situation, then replace every bracketed placeholder with real dates and names.
2. Always name a backup person for anything longer than a day — an out-of-office without a contact frustrates senders.
3. Set the reply before you leave, and test it by sending yourself an email from another account.
4. Turn the reply off on your first day back, and reply to waiting emails starting with the most urgent.
5. Save your favorite template in your email signature tool or notes app so next time takes seconds.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I say why I am away in an out-of-office reply?

Keep it general — 'on leave' or 'unavailable' is enough. You do not owe senders personal details, and specifics about health or travel are better kept private.

### How long should an out-of-office message be?

Short: three to four sentences. Return date, backup contact, and a thank-you cover everything a sender needs.

### Do I need an out-of-office reply for a single day off?

Yes, if people expect same-day replies from you. Even a one-day reply prevents follow-up emails asking why you have not responded.
