Your channel name is the first thing a viewer reads and the hardest thing to change later, so it deserves more than five minutes of thought. These one hundred ideas span the niches new creators actually start — cooking, tech, travel, fitness, gaming, finance, crafts, and more — and every one is original, pronounceable, and brand-friendly. Use them as they are, or treat them as starting points: swap a word, combine two ideas, or adapt one to your own style. The goal is a name that is easy to remember, easy to spell, and still feels like yours in a year. A name also sets expectations before anyone watches a video: it signals your topic, your tone, and whether a viewer belongs here. Take the time to choose well now, because renaming later costs recognition you have already earned. The right name feels obvious once you find it — short, clear, and unmistakably you.

## What's inside this resource

The ideas are grouped loosely by vibe rather than strict niche, because a good name often crosses categories. You will find cozy and calm names, energetic and bold ones, and clear descriptive names that tell viewers exactly what to expect. Short names dominate the list on purpose: two or three words are easier to say aloud, fit in a channel banner, and survive being typed into a search bar. Before you commit, check the name on YouTube, social platforms, and as a domain — a name that is taken everywhere will cost you more confusion than it is worth.

## How to use this resource

1. Shortlist five names you like, then say each one out loud — if it is hard to pronounce, viewers will not share it.
2. Check availability on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and as a domain before you fall in love with one.
3. Ask three honest friends which name they remember an hour later; the winner is usually the memorable one.
4. Avoid names tied to one trend, year, or platform feature — you want a name that still works in five years.
5. Once you choose, use the same name everywhere so viewers can find you on every platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my YouTube channel name later?

Yes, YouTube lets you change it, but you lose recognition each time. Viewers, links, and word of mouth all point to the old name, so choose carefully now.

### Should my channel name include keywords?

It can help search, but clarity beats keyword stuffing. A name people remember and can spell matters more than squeezing in a search term.

### What if my favorite name is already taken?

Adapt it: add a word that reflects your angle, like your city, your style, or your audience. A small twist keeps the idea while making it yours.
