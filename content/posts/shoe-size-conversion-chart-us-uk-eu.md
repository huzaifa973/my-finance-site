## How to measure your feet (do this first)

<!-- ADSENSE: in-article ad slot -->

Conversions are approximations — your foot length in centimeters is the ground truth.

1. Measure in the **evening**, when feet are at their largest.
2. Stand on a piece of paper (sitting under-measures) and trace around your foot holding the pen vertical.
3. Measure heel to longest toe in **centimeters**.
4. Do **both feet** — use the larger measurement.
5. Add **0.5–1 cm** of wiggle room for comfort (more for running shoes, less for dress shoes).
6. Match the cm number to the chart above or punch it into our free [shoe size converter](/tools/shoe-size-converter/) for an instant US/UK/EU result.

## Quick conversion rules of thumb

- **Men's US → UK:** subtract ~0.5 (US 10 ≈ UK 9.5).
- **Women's US → UK:** subtract 2 (US 8 ≈ UK 6).
- **US → EU:** add roughly 33–34 to men's US (US 9 + 33.5 = EU 42.5) — but use the chart, not math, at the edges.
- **EU → cm:** EU sizes are "Paris points" — each full size ≈ 6.67 mm of foot length.

## Why sizes still go wrong

**Brand lasts differ.** Nike, Adidas, and Clarks all shape their shoes on different foot molds. A US 9 in one brand can fit like an 8.5 in another. The brand's own size chart (with cm/insole length) beats any generic conversion.

**Width matters.** Standard charts assume medium width (D for men, B for women). Wide or narrow feet need the brand's width options, not just a length conversion.

**Shoe type changes fit.** Running shoes run small (size up half), dress shoes run narrow, and boots vary wildly. Read reviews for "runs small/large" notes before ordering.

**Kids' sizes are separate.** Children's US/UK/EU conversions follow different scales — do not extrapolate from adult charts.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a US size 9 in UK and EU?**
Men's US 9 ≈ UK 8.5 ≈ EU 42.5. Women's US 9 ≈ UK 7 ≈ EU 40. Always check the brand's own chart, since conversions vary slightly.

**Are UK and US shoe sizes the same?**
No. Men's UK sizes run about 0.5–1 size smaller than US (US 10 ≈ UK 9.5); women's UK sizes run about 2 sizes smaller (US 8 ≈ UK 6). EU sizes use a completely different numbering.

**How do I measure my foot for shoe size?**
Trace your foot on paper in the evening (feet swell during the day), measure heel-to-toe in cm, and match to a size chart. Leave 0.5–1 cm of wiggle room.

**Why do shoe sizes differ between brands?**
There is no enforced global standard — each brand uses its own 'last' (foot mold). Conversions are approximations; the brand's centimeter/insole measurement is the most reliable number.
