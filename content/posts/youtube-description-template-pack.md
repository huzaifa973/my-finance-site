The first two lines show up in search results, timestamps create clickable "key moments" in Google, and the full text tells the algorithm exactly what your video covers. ## How to use this pack

1. Pick the template that matches your video type — don't force a review structure onto a vlog.
2. Write the hook first: one line with the keyword and the payoff, because only the first 150 characters show in search.
3. Fill in real timestamps after editing (YouTube auto-links them) — they unlock "key moments" in Google results.
4. Add your links, then the disclosure line if any link is affiliate or sponsored — plain language, no hiding it.
5. Finish with 3-5 hashtags and one clear CTA; descriptions that ask for nothing get nothing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do YouTube descriptions affect rankings?**
Yes. The first 150 characters appear in search results, and keywords throughout the description help YouTube understand your topic. A strong description with timestamps also earns "key moments" in Google, which boosts clicks.

**How long should a YouTube description be?**
200-400 words is the sweet spot for most videos: enough for keywords, timestamps, links, and disclosures without fluff. Only the first 2-3 lines show before "show more," so front-load the hook and keywords.

**Do I need to disclose affiliate links in my description?**
Yes — it's required by the FTC and by YouTube's own policies. A plain one-line disclosure ("Some links are affiliate links...") keeps you compliant and actually builds viewer trust.
