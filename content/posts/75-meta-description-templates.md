It doesn't directly boost rankings, but it controls your click-through rate — and a great description can steal clicks from pages ranking above you. ## Frequently asked questions

**Do meta descriptions affect Google rankings?**
Not directly — Google has confirmed they are not a ranking factor. But they control your click-through rate, and a compelling description can win clicks away from higher-ranking pages, which is effectively the same as ranking higher.

**How long should a meta description be?**
Aim for 120 to 155 characters. Google typically shows around 155-160 characters on desktop; anything longer risks being cut off with an ellipsis, which weakens the message.

**Should every page have a unique meta description?**
Yes for every page that matters. Unique descriptions prevent Google from auto-generating bland snippets, and they let you match the exact search intent of each page's target keyword.
