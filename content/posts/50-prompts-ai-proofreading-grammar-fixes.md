## How to use these prompts

1. Copy the prompt that matches your goal and paste your draft where [TEXT] goes.
2. For important documents, run a mechanics prompt first, then a clarity prompt — fixing grammar before restructuring avoids wasted edits.
3. When you want light touch-ups, use the "correct only the grammar" constraint so the AI preserves your voice.
4. Ask the AI to list its changes; reviewing the list teaches you the pattern behind your repeat mistakes.
5. Always do one final human read for facts, names, and tone — AI proofreads language, not truth.

The prompts that teach beat the prompts that just fix. Use the ESL and explain-my-changes variants regularly and your first drafts will need fewer corrections within weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can AI reliably proofread my writing?**
Yes for grammar, spelling, and clarity — modern AI catches far more than a spellchecker, including wrong-word errors and tone issues. Always do a final human read for facts, nuance, and anything high-stakes like contracts or applications.

**How do I stop AI from rewriting my whole text when I only want fixes?**
Say so explicitly: "Correct only the grammar. Do not change wording, tone, or structure." Several prompts in this pack use that constraint, and it works reliably across the major AI tools.

**Will these prompts work in any AI chatbot?**
Yes. They are plain-text instructions with no tool-specific syntax, so they work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and any other conversational AI. Paste the prompt, paste your text, done.
