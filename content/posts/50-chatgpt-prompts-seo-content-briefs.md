Most SEO content fails not because the writing is bad, but because the brief was vague — the writer guessed at intent, skipped the SERP, and hoped for the best. These 50 prompts fix that. They turn any AI assistant into a tireless SEO strategist that researches keywords, reverse-engineers page one, plans outlines, bakes in E-E-A-T signals, and diagnoses decaying content. ## How to use this pack

1. Pick the group that matches your current job: keyword research for planning, outlines for new drafts, SERP analysis before writing, E-E-A-T for trust, refresh for old posts.
2. Copy a prompt and replace every bracketed placeholder with your real topic, keyword, URL, or draft text.
3. Run it in your AI tool of choice and review the output like an editor, not a reader — fix anything the AI hallucinated before briefing a writer.
4. Save winning outputs as templates: once a prompt produces a great brief, keep the filled version for the next article on a similar topic.
5. Chain prompts together: run a keyword prompt, feed its winner into an outline prompt, then feed the outline into a SERP-analysis prompt for a full end-to-end briefing workflow.

Used this way, the pack becomes a repeatable content system rather than a bag of one-off prompts. Most users find that three chained prompts — keyword, outline, SERP analysis — produce a brief strong enough that a decent writer's first draft needs only light editing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do these prompts work with free AI tools?**
Yes. Every prompt is written in plain instruction language with no reliance on paid features, plugins, or browsing. They work in the free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and similar tools, though results improve with more capable models.

**Should I let AI write the brief and the article?**
Use AI for the brief and keep a human in charge of the article. AI is great at research, structure, and gap analysis, but ranking content needs real experience, original insight, and careful fact-checking that only you or your writer can add.
