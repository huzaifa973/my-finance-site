Most self-published books don't fail because of the writing — they fail the formatting check. Readers forgive a lot, but not a book where chapter headings change size halfway through, images overlap the text, or the table of contents links to the wrong pages. Amazon's own quality checks will flag or even suppress books with serious formatting errors. And print is unforgiving: a margin that's fine on screen can swallow text into the gutter of a bound book. Formatting is the difference between a book that looks professional and one that screams "I did this in an afternoon."

This checklist covers the entire formatting process for both KDP ebook and KDP paperback, from manuscript cleanup to the final pre-upload review. It walks through stripping manual formatting, setting up proper styles, building front and back matter in the right order, handling images and tables, generating a working table of contents, and running the different export checks each format needs. It's built for first-time authors, indie publishers, and anyone preparing a manuscript in Word, Google Docs, or similar tools. Work through it in order — each stage builds on the last — and your book will pass KDP's checks on the first upload.

## What's inside this checklist

The 40 items are organized into six stages. Manuscript cleanup comes first: removing double spaces, manual indents, empty paragraphs, and stray formatting, then applying proper paragraph and heading styles throughout. Front matter covers the required order — title page, copyright page, dedication, epigraph, table of contents, foreword or preface — and what each page must contain, including the edition notice and rights statement.

Body formatting handles the details readers notice subconsciously: consistent chapter headings, first-line indents (but not on the first paragraph of a chapter), proper scene-break markers, smart quotes instead of straight quotes, and widow/orphan control for print. Images and tables covers resolution minimums, caption styles, and why complex tables need simplifying for ebooks. Back matter includes the about-the-author page, also-by listings, and review-request pages in the right positions. The final stage splits into ebook-specific checks (reflowable EPUB validation, TOC links, no fixed layouts) and print-specific checks (trim size, margins, bleed, embedded fonts, gutter allowance) so each file passes its own upload review.

## How to use this checklist

1. Finish your manuscript edits first — formatting a draft that's still changing means redoing the work.
2. Work through stages 1 to 5 in order on your master manuscript file; these stages are identical for ebook and print.
3. Duplicate the cleaned manuscript into two files — one for ebook export, one for print layout — then apply stage 6A to the first and 6B to the second.
4. For print, decide your trim size before touching margins or page numbers; changing trim size late forces a full re-layout.
5. Upload to KDP, order a physical proof copy for print and test the ebook on Kindle Previewer, and fix anything you spot before hitting publish.

Format once, format right — a clean file sails through KDP review while a sloppy one bounces back with flags.

## Frequently asked questions

**Word, Google Docs, or dedicated software — what should I format in?**

Word handles both ebook and print adequately for text-heavy books; Google Docs works for ebooks but struggles with precise print layout. For image-heavy or complex print books, dedicated tools like Atticus, Vellum, or InDesign are worth it. The checklist works regardless of tool.

**How do I handle the copyright page?**

Include the copyright symbol or word "Copyright," the year of first publication, and the rights holder's name. Add "All rights reserved," an edition notice, your ISBN for print if you have one, and a fiction disclaimer if the book is a novel. Keep it to one page.

**Why did KDP reject my print PDF?**

The usual culprits are missing embedded fonts, margins smaller than KDP's minimums for your page count, and a trim size mismatch between the file and your KDP setup. Run through stage 6B item by item — the fix is almost always in there.
