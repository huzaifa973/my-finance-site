Digital planners are one of the best beginner digital products: you create the file once, upload it to Etsy, and it sells while you sleep with near-100% profit margins. No printing, no shipping, no inventory. Here's exactly how to do it with free tools.

## Why digital planners work

A digital planner is a PDF designed for tablet note-taking apps (GoodNotes, Notability, Penly). Buyers pay $5–$25 for a file that costs you nothing to reproduce. Compare that to physical products, where every sale has a cost attached.

The market is real: students, professionals with ADHD, fitness trackers, budget planners, and wedding planners all buy specialized planners year-round, with spikes in December–January (New Year planning) and August (back to school).

## Step 1: Pick a specific planner niche

"Daily planner" has 50,000 competitors. These don't:

- **ADHD-friendly planners** — time-blocking with built-in break reminders, low-clutter layouts
- **Nursing/medical student planners** — clinical rotation trackers, drug reference pages
- **Budget & debt payoff planners** — savings trackers, bill calendars, no-spend challenges
- **Fitness planners** — workout logs, meal planners, habit trackers for specific programs
- **Wedding planners** — budget trackers, vendor comparison sheets, countdown checklists
- **Teacher planners** — lesson plan layouts, grading trackers, seating charts

Validate like this: search Etsy for your niche + "planner", check that results show 1,000–10,000 listings (demand with beatable competition), and confirm bestsellers have 100+ reviews. Then check Google Trends to make sure interest is stable or rising.

## Step 2: Design the planner in Canva (free)

1. Create a custom-size document (for GoodNotes, landscape 8.5×11 or iPad ratio works well).
2. Build these core pages: **cover, yearly overview, monthly calendars, weekly spreads, daily pages, and 3–5 tracker pages** specific to your niche (this is what makes it sell over generic planners).
3. Use 2–3 complementary fonts max and a consistent color palette — planners are bought on aesthetics as much as function.
4. Add **hyperlinked tabs**: in Canva, select tab text → link → page in the document. Tabs that jump between sections are the #1 feature buyers look for, and they're free to add.

Keep it to 30–80 pages. Bigger isn't better — focused and beautiful beats bloated.

## Step 3: Export, test, and package

1. Export as **PDF Standard** (not Print — smaller file, and hyperlinks stay active).
2. Test every tab link by clicking through the PDF yourself. One broken link = a 1-star review.
3. Test in a free note-taking app if possible — even the free tier of Penly or the GoodNotes trial confirms usability.
4. Create a **"how to use" instruction PDF** (one page: download, import into GoodNotes/Notability, start planning). This cuts support messages by 80%.

Your final product: the planner PDF + instructions PDF, zipped if needed (Etsy allows up to 5 files, 20MB each).

## Step 4: Create listings that convert

Your listing images sell the planner — buyers can't flip through it, so show them:

1. **Cover image:** the planner cover on a styled tablet mockup (Canva has free mockup templates).
2. **Feature spread:** weekly layout + tab navigation visible.
3. **Niche pages:** the 2–3 tracker pages unique to your niche.
4. **"What's included" graphic:** page count, hyperlinked tabs, compatible apps.
5. **How-it-works graphic:** buy → download → import → plan (kills purchase hesitation).

### Listing SEO

- **Title:** "ADHD Daily Planner, Undated Digital Planner for GoodNotes, Time Block Planner with Habit Tracker" — use all 140 characters with phrases buyers actually search.
- **13 tags:** every tag a buyer phrase ("adhd planner", "digital planner goodnotes", "undated planner").
- **Description:** lead with who it's for and the key features; include compatibility (GoodNotes, Notability, Penly, Xodo) and a note that it's a digital download with no physical item shipped.

### Pricing

Price digital planners at **$8–$18**. Below $5 you attract bargain hunters who leave picky reviews; above $25 needs a big page count and premium branding to justify. Check what the top 5 sellers in your niche charge and position in the middle — then run launch sales at 20–30% off to gather initial reviews.

## Step 5: Launch and get your first reviews

Publish **10–20 listings** before expecting traction — one planner in one niche rarely gets found. Variations multiply your surface area: same planner in 3 color themes = 3 listings; plus a "lite" version and a bundle listing.

Your first 10 reviews are everything. Ethical ways to get them: price 30% below market for the first month, deliver a flawless product, and include a polite review-request card in the download files ("Love your planner? A review helps my small shop enormously").

## Scaling: from one planner to a shop

Once a planner sells steadily:

1. **Bundle** — 3 related planners at a 25% discount raises average order value.
2. **Seasonal versions** — dated 2027 planners uploaded in October catch the New Year wave.
3. **Adjacent niches** — if your ADHD planner sells, an ADHD student planner reuses 70% of the work.
4. **Etsy Ads** — only after a listing converts organically; $1–$3/day on proven winners, never on untested listings.

## Mistakes beginners make

- **Copying bestsellers page-for-page** — Etsy buyers report copycats, and shops get shut down. Be inspired by structure, original in content.
- **Skipping the instructions file** — "how do I open this?" messages eat hours and tank ratings when buyers feel lost.
- **One listing and waiting** — Etsy rewards shops with depth. Ten listings minimum before judging results.
- **Ignoring December** — planner sales spike 3–5x in December/January. Have dated versions ready by November or miss the year's biggest wave.

### FAQ

**Q:** How much can you make selling digital planners on Etsy?
A: Beginners typically make $50–$300/month in the first 3–6 months with 20–50 listings. Established shops with 100+ listings in a good niche report $1,000–$5,000/month. Digital products have ~95% profit margins since there are no production or shipping costs.

**Q:** Do I need an iPad or paid software to make digital planners?
A: No. Canva's free tier is enough to design complete planners, and free PDF tools handle hyperlinking. An iPad helps for testing, but you can validate everything on a computer before spending money.

**Q:** Are digital planners oversaturated on Etsy?
A: Generic planners are saturated, but specific-use planners are not. 'ADHD daily planner', 'nursing student clinical planner', or 'wedding budget planner' each serve buyers that generic planners miss. Niche specificity is the entire game.

**Q:** How do customers receive a digital planner after buying?
A: Etsy delivers digital downloads automatically — the buyer gets instant access to the files after payment. You upload the PDF once, and Etsy handles every delivery forever. That's what makes it passive.
